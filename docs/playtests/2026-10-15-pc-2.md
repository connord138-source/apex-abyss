## The fix (4e0a67d, W7)

- **Cause:** `ChumService` `blocked()` measured each 6-stud column's dressing against that column's own Terrain height. Sand rising or falling under the footprint therefore scored as open floor. A Terrain reef arch over it would have too, since its top counts as that column's "floor".
- **Fix:** a column whose Terrain height is more than `LEVEL` (3) studs from the middle's now counts against the spot (+1 each; dressing still +3).
- **Re-test:** two Coral Reef whales, both on level sand. Trench and Open Blue whales still found spots (−426, and −918 in the canyon). No errors in that session.
