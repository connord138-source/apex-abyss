- **Sizes by rarity:** 15 / 17 / 19 / 21 px for Golden / Glowing / Crystal / Prism. Their light reaches about 9.8 / 12.6 / 15 / 17 studs.
- **Fades in:** the star's scale was off at 12 and 18 studs, then 0.18 at 24, 0.41 at 33 and about 0.75 from 48 on, twinkling.
- **Visible by distance** (a Golden Minnow by the hub, camera tracking it; kelp hidden on my screen for this series only, since kelp blocks every angle there):
  - 30: clear gold stars;
  - 60: visible but faint;
  - 100: barely;
  - 150 and 200: **not visible**.
  - Prism in open water in the Open Blue: the same pattern (a faint magenta smudge at 100, nothing at 200).
- **Why:** at 199 studs the glint was still on (Enabled, scale 0.52). Setting that one glint to draw on top for a moment showed a clear star at 200 studs (`G1-hub-golden-200studs-alwaysontop-test`). So the star is the right size: it's the water's haze, because the glint is depth-tested in the world (on purpose, so rock can hide it).
  - **For the world chat:** to read out to 240, draw it on top and hide it yourself when rock is in the way (a raycast to the camera, a few a frame), or give it a brighter, haze-proof look.
- **Rock hides it:** a solid block put between the camera and the fish at 40 studs hid the stars completely (`G1-occluder-*`).
- **Goes when eaten:** I ate the tracked Golden Minnow ("CAUGHT Minnow Golden"). Its fish went fully transparent and its glint was removed from the world.
- **No strays:** 19 stars showing, none on a hidden fish, none within 60 studs of the hub centre (the beacon rock), none past 240 from the camera. The plaza view shows none round the beacon (`G1-plaza-beacon-no-stray-stars`).
