# PC report: 2026-10-21 biomes (brief `2026-10-21-biomes-brief.md`)

Studio 0.744, `ApexAbyss.rbxl`. Screenshots: `docs/playtests/shots/2026-10-21-biomes/`.
I couldn't compare against `assets/tripo/biome_concepts/*Scene.png`, because they aren't on the
PC. I compared against `assets/concepts/biome_detail/Board*.jpg` instead.

## Summary

- **Fix 2551f74:** the landmark broadcast gets its own remote name. `Net` made two
  RemoteEvents named "Landmark", the broadcast and the request. The client's `WaitForChild` finds
  either, so in some sessions every bell bite, pearl bite and cannon entry went to the broadcast,
  which no server code hears. In this session the bell stayed silent and the Bay Cannon never
  fired. After the fix both work by hand.
- **Overlap: the Blue Hole's highway runs into the Kelp Cathedral.**
  - **Where it comes from:** `Shelf.luau` BLUE_HOLE searches near 37°, r 640 and picked 45°,
    r 590 (mark 417, 14, 417). Its 190-stud highway runs back toward the hub and ends near
    r 332 on 45°.
  - **The clash:** that's ~19 studs from the cathedral's centre (KelpDress CATHEDRAL 45°,
    r 345; nave ~165 across). Reef corals (a pillar, blue table, red fans) and the highway's
    carve stand in the nave's way in (`nave_with_reef_corals`).
  - **Even 37° / 640** would end the highway ~63 studs from the cathedral's middle.
  - **A placement call for the cloud:** move one landmark, or shorten the highway.
- **GiantKelp texture (place file):** its roughness map 98001769716451 was never approved, so
  every giant kelp drew grey. I cleared that map on `WorldProps.GiantKelp` and saved: the kelp is
  golden now. It's worth re-uploading it.

## BR: Coral Reef

- **Clam Garden:**
  - The view is good. A pearl bite gives "A pearl! 120 coral, 6 shells · it grows back".
  - A second bite at the same spot took the next clam's pearl, 12.5 studs away. The server's
    reach is 6 + Size.reach + slack.
  - Taken pearls grow back after ~15 min; until then, bites give nothing.
- **Garden eels:** top at 3.40 near and over them, rising to 6.09 after (they duck).
- **Blue Hole:**
  - Seen from the highway it matches the board: colour districts, light shafts, the hole beyond.
  - **Ripple arcs:** they read as thin pale lines; the brief says soft and darker.
- **The rim and below:**
  - Deep-blue Glacier walls darken down the shaft, with faint ring veils seen from above.
  - Mid-depth ledges carry corals, and the floor glows.
  - The tunnel (50°) is open both ways (0 stuck) and comes out at r 968.
- **PF at the rim:** 51.5 FPS.
  - LandmarkController 7.9 ms/s, KelpController 12.5, SchoolController 74.3,
    PredatorController 24.7, AmbientLife 6.6 (client total 242 ms/s).
  - LandmarkController is over ~5.

## Recommendations

- **The Blue Hole and the Kelp Cathedral:** decide which moves. The highway's end sits in the
  cathedral's way in.
- **KelpController:** look at it in the nave. The cathedral's ~665 kelp pieces in reach doubled
  it.
- **LandmarkController:** look at its ~7–8 ms/s near landmarks.
- **Ripple arcs:** make them softer and darker if the scene wants that.
- **Map labels:** move place names off the biome labels.
- **GiantKelp:** re-upload its roughness map, or leave it off.
