## 5. Build mode: snapping and starter layouts

**Setup:**
1. Admin Den: set the level 5.
2. Achievements: all done.
3. Den: things into the Build tray 2 (two of everything).
4. B in your den.

**B1 — snapping (on by default; the header reads SNAP ON):**
- **Turns:** R/F turn in 45° steps. G switches snapping off (SNAP OFF), and turns become 15°. G again turns it back on.
- **Neighbour alignment:** place a Driftwood Table, then aim a second floor thing about 0.5 studs off the first's x or z line. The ghost lines up exactly, and the status says "snapped".
- **Backing onto walls:** aim a Coral Lamp or a shelf a stud or two from the rock. The ghost backs onto the wall facing out.
  - Do the same against a pillar.
  - Do the same at the Hall's curved walls.
- **Wall things:** a second wall thing near the first lines up to its height.
- **Bad snaps:** any snap that puts something inside the rock, floating, or facing the wall is a fail. Screenshot each one.

**B2 — starter layouts:**
- **Each set:** pick a piece of the set in the tray (Q/E), aim at open floor, press L. Do this for each of the 7 sets:
  - Kelp Cottage, Reef Palace, Shipwreck, Sunken Temple, Vent Forge, Abyss Glow, Boss Trophies.
- **For each set, check:**
  - every floor piece of the set appears, arranged and facing you;
  - nothing overlaps (chairs on rugs are fine);
  - nothing hangs off a deck or stands in a hole;
  - wall and ceiling pieces stay in the tray.
- **Where:** try it in the Nook, the Hall, the Grotto and the Sanctum.
- **Two sets close together:** lay a second set right beside the first. It finds its own room.
- **Not enough room:** fill a small room, then L. You should get "Not enough open floor here".
- **Capacity:** with capacity nearly full, L lays out only as many as fit.
- **Moving:** move and put away pieces of a layout afterwards.
- **Aimed piece:** aim at a placed set piece with nothing picked, then press L. It lays out that set.
- Screenshot every set's layout.

**B3 — controller and phone:**
- Controller: D-pad up toggles snap, D-pad down lays out.
- Phone (Device Simulator):
  - the 7 buttons (PLACE, TURN, SNAP ON/OFF, LAY OUT, PUT AWAY, DROP, DONE) all fit, don't overlap, and work;
  - the tray isn't over the HAUL card.

## 7. Chum and the Whale Fall

**CH1 — chum from a death.**
1. Add Haul 50 kg.
2. Swim out to the Kelp.
3. Admin Get eaten.
- Respawned, swim back to where you died.
- There's a cloud of meat chunks and a red murk, sinking slowly. It's gone after about 45 s.
- With 2 clients:
  - client B eats client A, who carries a Haul;
  - the chunks appear where A died;
  - B (or A after respawning) swims into them;
  - each chunk pops "CHUM +x", and the Haul rises.

**CH2 — chum in front of you.**
- Admin "Chum in front of the target" 20.
- Chunks appear ahead. Swim through to eat them all.
- Do the suction and eat feel like shrimp?
- In a biome with predators (Coral Reef or Open Blue), burst 100 kg. Do the AI predators swim to the cloud within about 10 s?

**CH3 — Blood Tide slicks.**
- Admin Force a tide: Blood Tide. The chip reads "... chum in the water ...".
- Out in a biome, a small chum slick appears near you about every 20 s.

**CH4 — the Whale Fall.**
- Admin Whale Fall now.
  - A toast names the biome, and a pulsing W shows on the minimap and the full map.
  - About 5 s later the whale sinks (a big grey carcass with a bitten flank and ribs), the toast says it's sinking, and it settles on the floor.
- Swim there (Teleport to that biome, then follow the W).
  - 18 chunks hang round it; eaten ones grow back in about 14 s.
  - It's gone after 5 minutes, and the W clears.
- Separately: let a server run 90 s from start with no admin. The first natural Whale Fall comes, then one every 5 minutes in Studio.
- Screenshots: the warning, the sinking carcass, feeding at the floor, and a Trench Whale Fall (dark) if one comes up.
