## 2. The Megalodon

Summoned with Admin (`summonBoss`) in the Open Blue. My fish was LV 40.

To log exact distances, a script held my fish at a set gap from its body each frame. The gap uses the game's own measure: the distance to its axis, less 0.18 × its 130-stud length. The camera sat behind my fish, about 14 studs further out.

### B1 No see-through — PASS

- **Transparency**, read from the body mesh during the fight:
  - Client A, in front (it hunted A): 6 samples, all 0.0000, and its LocalTransparencyModifier read 0.0000 too. Every attack-event capture from the front read T 0.0000, wind-ups (jaw open) and bites alike.
  - Client A, beside: 3 samples, all 0.0000.
  - Client B, behind: 3 samples, all 0.0000. Exposure was +0.065 and atmosphere density 0.594 at the time.
- **By eye** (`01`–`05`): the body is opaque from every angle.
  - In front with the jaw open, the teeth and the inside of the mouth sit in front of a solid head. No far fin or tail lobe shows through.
  - Beside, the far pectoral fin and the tail are hidden behind the body.
  - Behind, the tail and dorsal fin are solid against the body. The pale rim outlines it.
- **Before and after** (`06`, last round's `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` next to this round's):
  - Last round the body was a ghost: you could see the jaw, the fins and the far side through it.
  - Now it's a solid animal. The jaw-open frame shows the red wind-up flash over a solid body.

## 3. The Giant Squid

Summoned with Admin after dismissing the Megalodon.

The canyon's rim is wider than the brief's "x ±150":
- `Seafloor.inTrench` measures the rim at width/2 + 300, about 380–405 studs either side of the canyon's line.
- The floor is width/2 + 60, about 165.

So below, "past the rim" uses the game's own rim.

### Q1 It rises in the Trench — PASS

- It rose at (46, −907, 1311): x 46 and z 1311, inside z 990–1430 and x ±150.
- The biome chip at the squid read **ABYSSAL TRENCH**. Screenshots: `18`, `19`, `20`.

### Q2 It stays in the Trench — PASS

- **The 2-minute watch** (positions every 10 s): x −51…70, z 1116…1332, y −878…−907. All on the canyon floor, near its line.
- **The fight minute:**
  - Jets landed at (−18, 1009) and (310, 1287), both inside the canyon.
  - While chasing and grabbing me (`21`), it went as far as x 465 at z ≈ 1100–1124, which is 96–115 past the rim. That is inside the 160 leash, so the leash allows it; it isn't on the canyon floor at that point.

### Q3 The leash — PASS

- **Led east** (toward the Ruins): my fish moved east at 25 studs/s and stopped at x 800.

  | t (s) | 5 | 15 | 25 | 36 | 41 | 46 | 51 | 56 | 61 | 71 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  - Furthest point: **(607, −420, 1203), 161 past the rim, 1,348 from the hub.**
  - On a second run it turned at (608, −420, 1199): 162 past the rim, 1,345 from the hub.
  - On that second run my fish then stayed right by it. Its next attack (a grab) came **7.7 s** after the turn.
- **Led north** (toward the hub): it followed to **(−2, −420, 958)**, r 958 (`23`).
