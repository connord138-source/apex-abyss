## 2. Test

**SS — start screen.**
1. Watch 10 cuts.
2. For each, note any moment you can see through the map or see unloaded water.
3. Copy any `[StartShots] … still loading` lines.
4. Press a key to dive in: the old intro scene should never show.

**DX — leaving a den.**
1. Leave your den 3 times.
2. The wash should hold until the plaza's rock is all round the fish, with no see-through floor or walls when it clears.
3. Time each exit.

**SX — bite sounds** (after the upload).
1. Bite open water: you should hear teeth clacking shut, not a hollow knock.
2. Bite a Barracuda: the crunch should play when the bite lands.
3. Say whether it reads as a bite.

**RL — rolls.**
1. Nibbler: admin **Skins: take every skin off the current fish**. Top up shells and roll 30 times.
2. Count the skins against coral. A fresh fish should get mostly skins at Common and Uncommon.
3. Open the odds panel. Check that it sums to 100%, and that Drowned Pearl (1 in 700) and Sunken Gold (1 in 1,500) are listed.
4. Roll again once a tier is owned: that tier's odds should move to its remaining skins.

**RC — rare fish and shells.**
1. Hunt 10 min in the Kelp, then 10 min in the Reef.
2. Count the Silver, Golden, Glowing and Crystal glints you see and the ones you catch.
3. Count the shells within ~150 studs of a tunnel mouth.

**CO — companions and medkelp.**
1. Admin **Sea turtle: one joins**: it should stay ~5 min.
2. Skim a resting stingray: it should stir up **+8 shells**.
3. With 5 Kelp Wraps (admin), swim through medkelp fronds: they should stay. With 4 wraps you should take one.

**RF — Coral Reef.**
1. Swim the Reef 5 min.
2. Every small fish over the coral should be edible, and the ones you eat should appear in the Catch Log.
3. List the kinds you see.
4. Open the Catch Log's biggest catches: sizes should vary visibly within a species.

**PV — PvP** 👥.
1. Admin: player A LV 50, player B LV 10.
2. Swim A into B: **nothing is swallowed**.
3. A bites B: count the bites to eat B (expect 3). Then B bites A: count the bites, or the damage per bite.
4. Fill A's Haul (admin) and bite again: the damage per bite should not change.
5. Check the nametags: B sees A as `LV 50 · STRONGER` in red, A sees B as `LV 10 · WEAKER` in green, and players within 5 levels as `EVEN FIGHT`.
6. Check the DANGER chip when A is near B.

**BS — bosses.**
1. Admin **summon** the Megalodon at LV 45.
2. Count the gill and tail bites to stagger (expect **12**), and the damage per bite (the boss bar's drop; expect ~12 of 6,000).
3. Estimate a solo kill time from the bites it takes to lose 10%.
4. Summon the Giant Squid at LV 85: count the eye or tentacle bites to stagger (expect **10**).

**WH — whales.**
1. Teleport to the Open Blue at LV 45 and find a humpback (they cross on 5 lanes, one every ~34 s).
2. Swim up to it: it should **stay in view** and you shouldn't pass through it.
3. BITE its flank: the first bite should show a toast (`A mouthful of whale · +… a bite`) and the Haul should grow.
4. Keep biting: by about 10 bites, or a full Haul, you should get "You're full of whale!".
5. With a boss summoned, the whales should be hidden.
6. Take a screenshot up close.

**WS — window skins.**
1. Wear Nebula (Reef Shark), Diamond (Nibbler) and Void (Moray).

   - no shimmer swimming straight;

**PR — predators.**
1. Barracuda at LV 16 (the Kelp's or the shelf's), playing the owner's way: dart in, bite, back off, repeat. It should bite you.
2. Sit still near it: it should strike within ~2 s.
