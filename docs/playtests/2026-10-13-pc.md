## The shafts (DN2): cause and a verified fix

- After a normal carve the Hall shaft's axis column reads Sandstone from den y 0 to −12 and the Sanctum shaft's from −36 to −44. Voxel reads round the Hall shaft's axis at y −6 after an admin 1→5 rebuild: every cell Sandstone 1.00 except four core cells at **0.21**, and no cell at 0 (`DN2-output-srv7-voxel-occupancy-after-normal-rebuild`). The Sanctum shaft keeps a 2-cell (8×8) open core with 0.37–0.75 round it.
- Why: Roblox's Air fills subtract occupancy rather than zeroing it. `DenCarve.ops` refills the shaft column with Sandstone (radius+2, ops #10/#11) and cuts it with one Air cylinder at the end (#62/#63); one r6 cut over fresh 1.00 rock leaves 0.21 in the core cells (they sit on a voxel corner: the axis is at world x 137.16, z 36.75, cell 136..140 × 36..40). Applying the identical cut a second time clears them (`DN2-output-srv6-one-cut-vs-two-cuts-occupancy`, `DN2-output-srv3-shaft-column-after-each-op`). It is not an ordering problem: applied one op at a time, #62 has no effect right after #61, and a frame yield before it changes nothing (`DN2-output-dn2e-box-sweeps-deeper-cut-vs-normal-build`).
- The cut's foot also doesn't break into the vault below: after a second cut, rays down the axes still stop at den y −11.0 (Hall) and −43.8 (Sanctum) (`DN2-output-dn2c-rays-after-second-cut`).
- Verified on the server (`DN2-output-dn2e-box-sweeps-r10-cuts`): cut each shaft from `floor+1` down to `ceiling(to) − 6` (4 studs deeper), **radius 10**, applied **twice**. A 6-stud box (the LV 60 root is 6×6×6) then sweeps the Hall shaft down to the storey-2 deck and the Sanctum shaft down to the Sanctum deck; an 8-stud box still clips at the top. With the current r6/r7, even a double, deeper cut passes only a 2-stud box (the opening is ~8 studs square and offset ~3 studs from the ring's centre). Baseline normal build: every box (2/4/6/8) stops at the top of both shafts. The curb rings would need to match the wider cut. In-game with the double cut the LV 1 fish swam into the Hall shaft (`DN2-level5-hall-shaft-after-second-cut-LV1-fish-in-hole`).
