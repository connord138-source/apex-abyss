## 5. Recommendations

1. **Trench prey trim: not needed now.** Keep the `fill` at ×1.9 for the Trench and canyon:
   - **No longer the slowest.** The Trench runs at **43 FPS on the PC** (round 1: 39) and **43 in
     the phone simulator** (round 2: 41), with a 26 1% low like the Kelp's 28 and the Reef's 29.
   - **The shadow switch was the big win.** It halved render prepare (11.9 → 5.7 ms) and cut 239
     draw calls. The Trench now renders lighter than the Kelp (render 11.3 ms against
     13.5–17.0).
   - **The prey are what's left, and a trim buys little.** They are now the Trench's main cost:
     round 2 measured 105 ms/s of SchoolController, ~2.5 ms a frame. I didn't re-profile the
     Trench; the brief asked for the Kelp only. Trimming ×1.9 → ×1.4 would buy ~1 ms.
   - **Studio Play hides render gains.** Trench FPS while swimming didn't rise even though render
     dropped 4–6 ms; standing still the frame was 18 ms (55/s).

   So Studio Play has stopped being a good judge of further render or prey cuts. Decide the trim
   on a real phone. If a mid-range phone is under target in the Trench, trim there first.
2. **Kelp sway:** done. KelpController went from the second-biggest client cost to the fourth
   (59 → 19 ms/s), and instances updated a frame fell from 576 to 192–320. In the Kelp the prey
   (SchoolController 70 ms/s, ForageController 36) are now the biggest script costs. Render
   prepare there is still 9–11 ms, the remaining CPU bottleneck.
3. **Diamond:** fixed in the den and the Trench, and much better in the Reef. If Connor wants
   the Reef to read as pale as the den, raise the skin's roughness a little or lift its glow map a
   touch. Either cuts the water's reflection. I'd leave it.
4. **Shadows off on the way down** costs one ~35–42 ms frame. I couldn't feel it, so I'd leave it.
5. **Normal maps already in the place:** 1,804 SurfaceAppearances at runtime carry a NormalMap,
   all on older Tripo props (den garden corals, shells, …). They were uploaded before the
   2026-10-13 rule, and CLAUDE.md already says re-import a bundle only when its model needs
   replacing. Nothing new was uploaded this round apart from the screened v15 patch (colour,
   glow, roughness/metal).
