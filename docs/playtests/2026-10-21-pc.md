## 3. Recommendations

- **Boss kill time:**
  - From the numbers above: ~12.5 a body bite, ~25 a gill bite, ×3 in a stagger.
  - Steady gill biting at about one a second, with staggers, takes ~2.5–3 min solo.
  - Body bites only take ~8 min.
  - Real fights with dodging land somewhere between, close to the 8–10 min target.
- **Whale feed:** 10 mouthfuls to "full" feels right, and the first-bite toast shows the amount.
- **Predator feel:** the Barracuda punishes sitting still (it bites in 1.5 s), as the brief
  wants. Someone should play the harass and juke loop by hand.
- **Shade on a variant:**
  - A shade's flat stand-in colour sometimes showed for 6–14 s before the tinted variant
    texture (parts-variants report).
  - `ShadeSkins` has no variant folders, so the "waiting" state shouldn't apply.
