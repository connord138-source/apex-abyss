## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

Connor's words are quoted with each change.

- **Wardrobe pose** ("The fish is facing the wrong direction in the skin selector"). The Wardrobe and Shade Dyer preview turns the fish head-left and toward you, like the roll cards. Before, it faced right and away.
- **Bite** ("left click is dash and right click is just a weaker dash"). Right click darts only when a fish, predator or boss is in reach. Into open water it is just the jaw's snap, with no forward lunge.
- **Pass panel** ("a free version of the deep pass ... and a visual showing the unlock tree"):
  - Two cards at the top: FREE PASS (OPEN, everyone has it) and DEEP PASS (LOCKED with a padlock; the card buys it).
  - Under them, a track with a node per tier.
  - Every reward shows as a lit 3D photo (the skin on your fish, coral, shells, wraps, a charm) or a badge (a den piece, a title, a map, a spin).
  - The Deep row is padlocked until it's bought.
- **Map place name** ("Map needs a name of the location they are at displayed"). A bar under the minimap reads YOUR DEN, THE HUB, the biome's name, or OPEN WATER.
- **Build mode editor** ("set them in a locked screen where they can edit the den and drag parts there. Its too difficult to do as a fish"):
  - B in your own den now takes the screen. The fish holds still, the camera looks down on the den's floor from three-quarters, and the cursor is free.
  - **PC:**
    - Click the floor to place.
    - Press on a placed thing, drag it, and let go to put it down. A quick click picks it up and the next click puts it down. Let go somewhere refused and it goes back.
    - Drag a thing straight out of the tray onto the floor.
    - RIGHT-DRAG orbits the view, WASD / arrows pan, the wheel zooms, SPACE / C go up / down a storey.
    - R F turn, G snap, L lay out a set, X put away, B done.
  - **Controller:** left stick pans, right stick orbits, RT places at the screen's middle, A goes to the next storey, B done.
  - **Phone:** a tap places or picks up, a drag orbits, a pinch zooms, and the FLOOR button changes storey.
  - The view stays inside the storey's rooms, under its ceiling (zoomed out it lowers toward the floor) and out of the rock.
- **Easier swimming** ("adjustments need to be made to make movement a tad easier"):
  - The fish gets up to speed a little quicker, stops sooner when you let go, and turns a little tighter at speed.
  - A camera tipped less than 12° up or down now swims level, so looking around no longer drifts the fish up or down. Past 12° the climb eases in, and straight up is still straight up.
- **Moving skins** ("you only made 2-3 skins interactive ... lava should have texture that looks like lava gaps and crystal should have a crystal texture ... Still too many flat models"):
  - Every Rare-and-up skin (and the Golden catch skin) now slides a moving layer over the fish, and most of them also give off particles.
    - Lava seeps through Magma's cracks.
    - Crystal and ice glint.
    - Sunlit ripples cross the sea-glass, reef, wreck and coral skins.
    - A current rakes back over Chrome, Riptide, Undertow, Abyssal Current, Mandarin, Ribbon, Wave, X-Ray and Abyss Ink.
    - Live arcs crackle on Thunder.
    - Gold glints run over the gold skins.
    - Stars drift on Comet and Starfall.
    - A tinted sparkle plays over the glow and pearl skins.
  - The skins that reuse the four layers already uploaded move straight away. Five new layers (lava, caustic, facets, current, bolt) need uploading first (step 3); until their ids are in, those skins stay still.
  - **Magma and Crystal are retextured**: Magma has real lava cracks between basalt plates; Crystal is cut crystal. They come as small patch packs (step 2) holding only the changed skins, so only a few images are uploaded.

## 1. Sync

1. Merge `origin/claude/world` into `claude/core-systems`.
2. Run `rokit install`, then `rojo serve`, and connect the plugin.
3. Format with `stylua --line-endings Windows src --glob "!**/Packages/**"`.
4. Play once. The Output should show no errors.

## 2. Fetch, screen and import the patch skins

1. Run `python tools/fetch_assets.py`.
   - It should print `fetched shade_patch_v9: 6 file(s) -> assets/shades/patch (sha256 db7d971c)`.
   - If it refuses the bundle over a sha256 mismatch, stop and report it.
2. Screen the six patch packs (nothing is uploaded by this). Run it with the classifier on; it takes a while:

   ```
   python tools/shades/screen_shades.py assets/shades/patch/Nibbler_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch/Cuda_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch/Puffer_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch/MorayEel_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch/ReefShark_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch/Angler_ShadesPatch.glb --quiet
   ```

   - It must exit 0 with nothing flagged. The cloud's run: 31 images, none flagged, highest classifier 0.021.
   - If the classifier can't load on this PC, re-run with `--no-classifier`, say so in the report, and carry on only if that exits 0.
   - If anything is flagged, stop and report the output. Don't import it.
3. In `ApexAbyss.rbxl` (which already holds the full skin packs), File → Import 3D the six `assets/shades/patch/<Fish>_ShadesPatch.glb` files **one at a time**, a few minutes apart for moderation. Import only these; don't re-import any `_Shades.glb`.
4. Paste `tools/studio/organize_shades.luau` into the Command Bar and Run.
   - It should print `filed 7 skin(s) for 6 fish` (CatchCrystal on each fish plus the Moray's Magma).
   - Check `ReplicatedStorage.ShadeSkins.MorayEel` still holds all its other skins (about 28), not just the two new ones.
5. Save (Ctrl+S).

## 3. Upload the five new layers

1. Screen them first (nothing is uploaded by this):

   ```
   python tools/shades/screen_shades.py assets/textures/sheen_lava.png assets/textures/sheen_caustic.png assets/textures/sheen_facets.png assets/textures/sheen_current.png assets/textures/sheen_bolt.png
   ```

   It must exit 0 (the cloud's run: classifier 0.000–0.002 on all five). If it doesn't, stop and report the output.
2. In Studio: Asset Manager → Import (as with the sound pack), add these five files from `assets/textures/` as Images, and Start Import:
   `sheen_lava.png`, `sheen_caustic.png`, `sheen_facets.png`, `sheen_current.png`, `sheen_bolt.png`.
3. Right-click each row in the Import Queue → Copy AssetId, and paste each id into `src/shared/Config/Textures.luau` in place of its `0` (`sheenLava`, `sheenCaustic`, `sheenFacets`, `sheenCurrent`, `sheenBolt`).
4. Commit that as "Sheen ids v9" and push.
