## How the pacing checks were driven

The fills were swum by a bot (`seek`): it swims the fish straight at the nearest fish it can swallow, at the fish's own top speed, and lets the game's own suction eat it. It never pauses, so **its fill times are a floor**: a person steering by eye will take longer. The bot's fish could still be eaten (Barracudas in the Kelp, Reef Sharks and the Megalodon in the Open Blue), and those deaths are noted where they happened.
