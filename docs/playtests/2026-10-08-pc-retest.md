## B2 Giant Squid

- **Summon:** needs the boss picker set to Giant Squid. "A boss is already up" if the Megalodon is on its clock; Dismiss first.
- **Grabs:**
  - Near the squid, in the Coral Reef shallows at y≈10: grabbed at once. "GRABBED · MASH BITE TO BREAK FREE" in red, HeldBy set, HP draining ~1.5 per 0.1 s (15%/s).
  - Released after 4 s on its own; a "TENTACLE · GET CLEAR" ring tell (1 s) came first.
- **Rising to meet you:**
  - Placed 180 above the floor and 60 out from it, I waited 12 s. It went **down and away**: its y went −26 → −51 → −78 → −104 → −130 → −156 while the distance grew 313 → 417.
  - It was likely heading back to its southern band, not hunting me.
- **Three grabs in about 25 s** in the Ruins and Trench water, with HeldBy at +59.4 s, +67.8 s and +73.2 s.
- **Two grabs back to back kill a full fish:**
  - Grab 1 took HP 100 → 41.
  - Grab 2 started 4.4 s later at HP 47 and drained to 0, and I was eaten.
  - The design says "nothing one-shots", but two chained grabs with no gap is close to it.
- **Breaking free:**
  - Keyboard presses faster than one per 0.12 s are dropped by the `Struggle` rate limit. My first mash (10 instant presses) didn't free me.
  - At 0.15 s spacing: **6 Struggles in 0.95 s freed me** (HP 45 → 33.5), and again 6 in 0.97 s.
  - A player mashing faster than ~8/s will be punished. Suggest counting presses on the client and sending a batch, or relaxing the limit while held.
- **Dark glow:** the squid carries a violet `Highlight` "Jaw" (outline 0.67 transparent, enabled) and a `PointLight` "Glow" (range 120, brightness 0.96). At 94 ft in the Trench it showed only as a faint grey blob: no clear violet outline, no visible pulse.
- **Not seen:** ink, jet and whirlpool. The squid stayed in LURKER the whole time, and I didn't get it below 65% for Enraged.
