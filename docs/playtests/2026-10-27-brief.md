## 2. What changed

| Fix | Where |
|---|---|
| **Reef stepping** (your PJ1): a fish's move rate now goes by how fast it really moves on screen, measured as it's drawn (how far it went plus its nose and tail's turn since its last move, a peak that settles over ~0.5 s), never its configured `speed`. A Butterflyfish that sways at ~20 studs/s is moved every frame out to ~150–200 studs. | `SchoolController` (`pace`, `PACE_SETTLE`, `PACE_GAP`) |
| **The per-fish loop** (`step`'s own body, 35–42 ms/s): a held fish costs one compare (`holdUntil`); the eat check runs only for fish near your mouth (`eatLook`); the wave and the tail beat are only worked out where they're used. | `SchoolController` |
| **Raft fish are little fish**: under every raft and round the pinnacle they're small Sardine and Minnow models (1.8 studs) instead of coloured boxes. | `LandmarkController` (`SHELTER_PREY`) |

The cloud's prey density sim (`run_density.sh`) now measures each fish's pace off its own path (Butterflyfish 22 studs/s against `speed` 5, Blue Tang 31 against 6, Opaleye 12, Lionfish 10, tuna 19). With the new rule, moves a frame are Kelp 192, Reef 189 and Open Blue 148. That's still well under the 355 / 311 / 291 before the tiers, though above last round's 128 / 110 / 106.
