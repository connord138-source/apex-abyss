## 2. The Megalodon

Summoned with Admin (`summonBoss`) after a teleport to the Open Blue. My fish was LV 40.

To log exact distances, a script held my fish at a set gap from its body each frame (the same body measure the game uses: its axis, less 0.18 × its length). The camera sat 14 studs behind my fish, facing the boss. So the camera's gap is about the fish's gap plus 14, and the fade goes by the camera's.

  | Above the floor | 257 | 243 | 273 | 296 | 292 | 303 | 313 | 294 | 330 | 336 | 339 |

- **The warning** starts just inside 460 (at exactly 460 the dread is still 0). Until the boss starts to show it has no arrow or distance.
- **The edges** darken and pulse with the heartbeat (vignette down to 0.58).
- **The centre doesn't get darker.**
  - The small exposure dips between rows come from depth: the boss moves and the camera's height follows it.
  - Same camera spot, boss present (dread on, edges at 0.72): exposure **0.066**, grade brightness **0.025**.
  - Same spot, boss dismissed: exposure **−0.004**, grade brightness **0.019**.
  - Neither goes down with the dread. The code has no dread term in either; the dread only greys the tint and beats the contrast.
- **Screenshots:** `02` (460), `03` (400), `04` (250), `05` (150), `06` (80).
- **How it reads:** an eerie figure fading into view, yes.
  - The warning and the pulsing edges build tension with nothing to see.
  - Then a dark shape gathers out of the blue.
  - By ~80 studs it's a huge grey silhouette, its outline caught by the pale rim (`06`).
  - The fade is smooth, with no pop.
- **Two notes on timing:**
  1. **The HUD gives it away early.**
     - The boss bar and "MEGALODON NEARBY · 230 ft" with the arrow come on at 280 (measured from the fish).
     - The body fades by the camera's distance and eases in, so at 250 it is still 98% see-through.
     - For ~50–80 studs the HUD says it's here while nothing shows.
     - Suggestion: switch the bar and NEARBY when it's ~30% faded in, or measure both from the same point.
  2. **The haze hides the first half of the fade.**
     - At 150 the body is 76% opaque but hard to make out at ~490 ft (`05`).
     - It reads as a shape from ~110 and clearly by ~80.
     - If it should be seen sooner, a lower `far` won't help; a lighter haze near it would.

## 7. The feel

- **The Megalodon reads as an eerie figure out of the deep blue.**
  - The approach is felt before it's seen: the warning with no distance, the beating edges and the cold grey water.
  - Then a dark shape gathers out of the blue and becomes a huge silhouette with a pale rim.
  - Two things blunt it:
    - the boss bar and "NEARBY · distance" appear ~50–80 studs before anything shows;
    - the haze eats the first half of the fade, so it effectively appears between ~120 and ~80 studs.
  - Syncing the HUD to the fade would make the reveal land.
- **Both bosses are comfortable to fight now.**
  - The Megalodon is plainly visible from every side for both players, the opposite of last round's black screen, and its tells read.
  - The squid is visible in the Trench's dark with its glow and rim, the dark lifts as you close, and the ink is a short, fair blind.
- **What makes them hard to fight where intended:**
  - The squid rises in the Ruins and roams far.
  - The Megalodon's last stand never reaches the Trench.
