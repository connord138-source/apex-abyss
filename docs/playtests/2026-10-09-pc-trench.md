- **Ranges match the config.**
- **The holder's view lifts with rarity:** OutdoorAmbient (32 → 41, 58 → 77, 68 → 94) and Brightness (0 → 0.65).
- **No Common glowing shade exists.** Config/Shades only marks Rare and up as `emissive` (Lunar, Glowspot, Lanternfish, Magma, X-Ray, Aurora, Thunder, Prismatic, Nebula, Void, Ghost, Abyss Ink), so the `Common` source (range 18) is never used.
- **Lantern plus a glowing shade:**
  - With a Deep Lantern running, wearing Magma gives Lamp 36 / 1.66 with no beam: the shade wins.
  - Wearing Glowspot gives Lamp 30 / 1.60 with the lantern's beam. Glowspot's reveal (0.10) only ties the lantern's, so the lantern stays.
  - Both as coded (shot 18).
- **Two players (Local Server):** Player2 wore Magma at the Trench bottom. Player1's client, 30 studs away, had Player2's `Lamp` at range 36, brightness **1.90**, and saw Player2 glowing with an orange pool on the floor (shot 19).
- **Finding 1: the holder's own shade is dimmed by its own reveal.**
  - `LampController` scales a shade's brightness by `0.2 + 0.8 × darkness`, and `OceanController` makes `darkness = target × (1 − reveal)`.
  - So the holder's own shade gets ×0.92 (Rare), ×0.87 (Epic), ×0.79 (Legendary), ×0.73 (Mythic), ×0.70 (Trophy).
  - The logged brightnesses fit exactly: 1.29 / 1.66 / 2.07 / 2.34 / 2.37 instead of 1.4 / 1.9 / 2.6 / 3.2 / 3.4.
  - Others see the full value, because their own reveal is 0 (Player1 saw Magma at 1.90; the holder had it at 1.66).
  - The top three tiers end up within 0.3 of each other on the holder's own screen. Scaling by the water's darkness before reveal would fix it.
- **Finding 2: colour changes how strong a light feels.**
  - Magma's orange pool looks brighter than Aurora's teal-green, so the Epic reads stronger than the Legendary.
  - Abyss Ink's deep violet reads darker than Nebula's purple, though they're the same tier and range.
  - Ghost's pale green pool is the widest-looking.
- **The Anglerfish shot isn't a fair comparison:** switching species reset it to LV 1, so the fish is tiny on screen. Its values are in the table.
