**Why single items are hard to eat:** for the 1 kg starter the eat reach is `Size.reach(2) + 0.4` ≈ 1.24 studs around a mouth 1 stud ahead of the body. That's tight for players chasing single shrimp, which hop. Consider a more generous reach for forage, or a slight pull toward the mouth.
