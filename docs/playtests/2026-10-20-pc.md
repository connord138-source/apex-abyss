## Recommendations

1. **FPS:** check the Kelp and the Trench on a real phone. If they're under 45 there, trim
   `Config/Prey` `fill` for those zones, or `perSchoolMax`, before the next owner batch.
2. **The tutorial's Haul** fills during step 2's ten fish, so step 4 is skipped on sight. Either
   step 2 eats fewer fish, or step 4's target counts from the start of the step.
3. **Phone tutorial card:** the idle stick ring (100, H−74) overlaps the bigger card's key caps.
   Lift the ring, or move the card up by the ring's radius.
4. **Diamond on the Nibbler** reads almost white in den light. It could use a deeper body under
   the facets, or a lower glow multiplier.
5. **Window skins on fish:** a quick in-game look by Connor at Nebula on the Reef Shark,
   swimming and turning, would settle WS2. The formula is right on part faces.
6. **Manta testability:** a `Draft` character attribute while `SwimController.draft` is true
   (or a print) would let a playtest confirm ×1.3 without hand-aligning the camera.
7. **Den geometry:** the carved vault is 3–4 studs under DenSpace's analytic vault. Checks like
   `check_doors.luau` that use the analytic one will pass things the real rock cuts.
