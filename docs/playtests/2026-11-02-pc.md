# PC report: 2026-11-02 (brief `2026-11-02-brief.md`)

Studio 0.745 (it updated itself since last round), `ApexAbyss.rbxl` on `claude/core-systems` at b23a2e8
plus the `Net.luau` fix below, Rojo 34873. Screenshots: `docs/playtests/shots/2026-11-02/`.

## Summary

- **No blocker left after the `Net.luau` fix, so I'm publishing** (PlaceVersion below).
- **One blocker, found and fixed: the den expansion's requests weren't registered.** `DenWing`,
  `DenOpenPlan`, `AquariumStock`, `AquariumCollect` and `AquariumFeed` were missing from `Net.luau`'s
  `REQUESTS`, so `DenService.start` asserted and the server's service start stopped there. In Play:
  - `ServerScriptService.Server.Net:342: unknown request DenWing`;
  - `Infinite yield possible on ReplicatedStorage:WaitForChild("WheelState")`.

  Nothing after DenService started (no wings, no tanks, no wheel). I added the five entries, with
  rate limits like their neighbours (0.5 / 0.5 / 0.25 / 1 / 1), and formatted with StyLua. Every check
  below ran with the fix.
- **The den expansion works.** Wings carve in all four doorways with refunds exactly as priced. The
  LV 100 shark and the LV 1 Nibbler swim every wing and passage with no stuck spots. Tanks stock,
  earn, collect and feed. Open Plan opens and closes for free after the first buy. Old saves load.
  Findings:
  - **DE14:** AquariumController costs **43–45 ms/s** (aim under ~5). FPS is still 60.
  - **DE5:** the Trophy Gallery doesn't refresh when a new catch record comes in. It only picks the
    record up on a full den rebuild.
  - **DE7:** a passage's mouth takes placements. The brief expects a red ghost there.
- **BF1 fails on look.** The Teal Brazier's fire emitters sit 0.6 studs up a 9.6-stud model, under
  its bowl. The model draws white because Studio 0.745 refuses its SurfaceAppearance ("forbidden
  access", below).
- **Den hearts work with two players (DE13).** An admire tips the owner and adds a heart. The count
  shows on the plaza nameplate, the title board inside and the den menu. A second admire the same day
  is refused. The admin rows add 5 hearts and put the gold frame on both signs, and the other player
  sees it too.
- **CA1: Golden passes, Silver falls short.** In 8 minutes: 6 Silver and 4 Golden caught. The server
  turned only 9 Silver rare for me (~1.1 a minute), so the Silver stream, not the chase, is what fell
  short.
- **PA1 (quick):** every part sits as the brief describes on all six fish.
- **Studio 0.745 adds a new red error**, "Failed to load … due to forbidden access. PBR textures may
  not be visible in game". It names the Charm Keeper's prop, the Whale's Rest skeleton, the Teal
  Brazier, the card warm-up skin, and my own fish's skin (the Moray's SurfaceAppearance, the Angler's
  ShadeSkin; the Angler drew white). It's new today and isn't in the game's code. Worth checking in a
  live server before reading anything into it.
- **No `[SpeedCheck]` line all session**, in any of the four logs (whaleGo, goTo, den exits and the
  two-player test included).
- **Tides:** back on the clock (`forceTide me ""`).
