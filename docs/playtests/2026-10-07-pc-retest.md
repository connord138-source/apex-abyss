**Still worth fixing:**

1. **Controller stick stays stuck after release (test 7).**
   - In the emulator, when you let go of the left stick the fish keeps swimming at full speed. In one run it drifted 710 studs out of the hub over about 2 minutes. A sideways stick keeps the camera spinning.
   - The emulator sends `InputChanged` (0, 1) then `InputEnded` (0, 0), with no final `InputChanged` near 0. `Input.luau`'s `InputEnded` handler never resets `stick1`/`stick2`. They only clear on `WindowFocusReleased`.
   - **Suggested fix:** zero the stick in `InputEnded` for `Thumbstick1`/`Thumbstick2`.
   - A real pad may send a near-zero `InputChanged` first, but the reset is cheap insurance either way.
2. **The phone stick ring appears below the thumb (test 11).**
   - It sits about 57 px lower than the touch. That matches the 58 px top-bar inset (`GuiService:GetGuiInset()` = 0, 58).
   - A touch low in the zone puts the ring half off the bottom of the screen.
   - Likely cause: `screenPoint` in `HudController.luau` adds `Ui.topInset()` to a touch position that already includes it, at least in the Device Simulator. Worth checking on a real phone.
3. **Roblox's own touch thumbstick also draws a ring.**
   - `PlayerGui.TouchGui.TouchControlFrame.DynamicThumbstickFrame` is visible. Its `ThumbstickStart` ring (74 × 74) sits next to the game's ring at rest (76 × 76).
   - It stays at rest while the game's ring moves under the thumb, so there are two rings on screen.
4. **A pure sideways stick orbits the camera around a fish that just coasts (controller and phone).**
   - With the stick straight sideways there is no throttle, so the fish brakes to a stop while the camera swings round it. On the phone that was about 149°/s (`steerMax` 2.6 rad/s), a full circle in about 2.4 s.
   - The fish and camera only swing round together when the stick is also pushed up (up-right: about 80°/s, camera within 5–6° of the fish).
   - **Your call** whether sideways alone should also turn the fish.
5. **Phone layout overlaps** (taps still work through all of them):
   - Outside the hub, the "KELP SHALLOWS · LV 1–10" chip sits on the top half of the BITE button, label included (`C21`, `C22`).
   - Near a stall, the "TAP TO SHOP" chip is drawn behind the HAUL card (`C25`).

  | Time | 0.0 s | 1.0 s | 1.5 s | 2.0 s | 3.0 s | 4.0 s | 5.0 s | 6.0 s |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Stick (gamepad state) | 0 | **1.0** | 1.0 | **0** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
  | Speed (studs/s) | 1.9 | 14.6 | 18.7 | 17.6 | 20.4 | 20.4 | 20.2 | 20.3 |

  The table is the clean check `LOG[release-check]`: W held for 1 s, then let go (`B12`, `B13`).
  - About 2 minutes later the fish was at (−413, 0.3, 582), 710 studs from the hub centre, still moving at 16.9.
  - The same happens after a sideways push: the fish kept circling at about 80°/s, and at rest the camera kept spinning.
  - After the right stick, the camera kept turning (J) or stayed pinned at −80° pitch (K).
