## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Whale plume (W1 failed):** the plume is now a dense column of pale silt, faintly self-lit, about 3× the puffs and bigger, rising about 150 studs so it clears the kelp.
- **Build camera (D6):**
  - Your own fish is hidden while you build.
  - The view starts in the middle of the room the fish is in, not over the pool.
  - Zooming out now climbs toward the vault and looks down. Once the camera is up under the vault, more zoom widens the lens (up to 96°).
- **Build mode hides the HUD on every device** (it was phone-only). On PC the top chips covered the view, and a click under a chip or the minimap was swallowed, which is the likely cause of D6's silent misses. Any placement that gets no answer within about a second now says "<thing> didn't go down · try again" on the bar.
- **Den walls (T1):** the dens' rock now uses Rock's plates (not Sandstone's bands) in the same tan. It's a new material entry, Limestone, that reuses the Rock image, so **nothing is uploaded**, but `make_toon_materials.luau` must be run again (step 2). The Mosaic floor is toned down, Marble is less bright, and the Basalt colour is lifted so the Vents' floor shows its tile. The Trench stays dark on purpose.
- **Skins drawing white (K1):** a fish keeps a stand-in look (its own texture tinted toward the shade) until all of a skin's textures have downloaded. A failed download is retried, a little later each time. After 12 s the skin goes on anyway, and it's put on again once the downloads finish.
- **Depth gauge (D8):** in a den it reads the depth in front of your doorway.
- **Stacks (B4):**
  - Moving a piece carries whatever is stacked on it, turned with it.
  - Click (tap, RT) a stacked piece to pick it up, then click again within about half a second: it picks the piece underneath instead (with its stack).
  - Tray tiles keep their place for the rest of the Build session; one that runs out stays greyed at ×0.
- **Smaller:**
  - On a phone with a menu window open, messages show on the window's subtitle line for 3 s, not as a strip. In Build mode on a phone they sit under the help line at the top.
  - Within the Treasure Wheel's reach, E / Y is the wheel's, unless you're right at a stall's counter (6 studs).
  - Menu rows no longer rebuild while a pad A or the mouse button is held, so the first A on the Shop's Log row should work.
  - The den's lamp cords end at the real rock (no poking through the roof).
- **Admin "Den: simulate an old save"** for D7: it moves your den's things to the heights they'd have had in a save from before the private dens, then loads them the way an old save loads.
- **Skins v11:** WeekWave and WeekWreck are retextured much lighter, with a faint glow so the water can't drag them dark, on all six fish (step 3).

## 1. Sync

1. Merge `origin/claude/world` into `claude/core-systems`.
2. Run `rokit install`, then `rojo serve`, and connect the plugin.
3. Format with `stylua --line-endings Windows src --glob "!**/Packages/**"`.
4. Play once. The Output should show no errors. **One warning is expected** until step 2: WorldService's note that a toon material (Limestone) has an id but no variant.

## 2. Den rock (no upload)

1. Stop play. In **edit mode**, paste `tools/studio/make_toon_materials.luau` into the Command Bar and Run. It should print `[make_toon_materials] 12 toon materials on, 0 still stock (id 0). Now File → Save.`
2. Save (Ctrl+S).

## 3. Skins v11 patch

In the same `ApexAbyss.rbxl` (it already holds the full skin packs and the v9 and v10 patches):

1. Run `python tools/fetch_assets.py`. It should print `fetched shade_patch_v11: 6 file(s) -> assets/shades/patch11 (sha256 485e3d85)`.
2. Screen the six patch packs with the classifier on (nothing is uploaded by this). It must exit 0; if it doesn't, stop and report the output.

   ```
   python tools/shades/screen_shades.py assets/shades/patch11/Nibbler_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch11/Cuda_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch11/Puffer_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch11/MorayEel_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch11/ReefShark_ShadesPatch.glb assets/shades/patch11/Angler_ShadesPatch.glb --quiet
   ```

3. File → Import 3D the six `assets/shades/patch11/<Fish>_ShadesPatch.glb` files **one at a time, a few minutes apart** for moderation. Each holds WeekWave and WeekWreck: 6 images (2 colour maps, 2 glow maps, 2 roughness maps), 36 in all. Don't re-import anything else.
4. Paste `tools/studio/organize_shades.luau` into the Command Bar and Run. It files only these 12 skins and keeps every other skin.
5. Save (Ctrl+S).
