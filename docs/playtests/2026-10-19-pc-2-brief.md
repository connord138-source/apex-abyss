## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Pacing** (PG1: a full Haul was +12 levels in 16–30 s, and nothing capped it on the server):
  - **A real Haul cap.** It is measured in levels and shrinks with level: 7 up to LV 3 (the tutorial still goes LV 1 → 8), then 0.75 of a level at LV 8 down to 0.2 at LV 100. MAX SIZE now means full, and everyday food stops adding Haul there. Eating still heals and still counts catches, DNA and coral.
  - **A Haul rate by level**, so a dive takes 2.5–4 minutes of hunting plus the swim home.
  - **Bonus meals are sized as shares of your own full Haul:** bait, whale chunks, Blood Tide slicks, predator loot, player kills, boss loot and treasure.
  - **Rewards go past the cap:** boss loot, treasure, admin kg and a Second Chance restore.
  - **Banking:** a bank shows how far you've got toward the next level, for example "40% OF THE WAY TO LV 26".
  - **Monetization:** Double Haul is offered on a Haul at least 75% full. Second Chance is offered when you lose at least half a full Haul.
  - **Sim targets** (`tools/tests/run_progress_sim.sh`):

    | Level | 15 | 25 | 40 | 55 | 70 | 85 | 100 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | Active play | ~36 min | 1.6 h | 4.1 h | 7 h | 10.8 h | 15.9 h | 21.9 h |

  - **Food below you counts for less:** past the top level of the biome you're in, prey and filler food give ×(1 − 0.1 per level over), never under ×0.2. The Haul card says "Too easy here: deeper water feeds you better". This stops the Open Blue's tuna being a fast lane.
  - **Treasure maps from prey** are capped at 7 an hour per player. Heavy prey had made them 30–65 an hour.
  - **Small gains read in grams**, for example "+56 g Haul", in the pops and the kill, pod, bait and boss texts.
- **Ruins head (RU1):** the bust lay inside a pediment half, which hid its face. It now lies on the back of its skull outside the left colonnade, face 40° up and gazing down the avenue. `HEAD_TURN` in `Ruins.luau` settles it in one line if it's still wrong.
- **Ruins prop count:** the brief's 47 came from the offline preview, which rolls its own random numbers. One outlying ruin was really missing in game; outlying ruins now retry at looser spacing. Expect about 40–42 hero props.
- **Forced tides (RT1):** a forced tide now lasts one whole tide from the moment it's set, including its opening boost. TideService clears it when it ends.
- **Prism Tide (RT2):** Prism rate 0.08 (was 0.10), and Silver is no longer boosted in the Prism Tide. The sim gives ~1.8 Prism a tide.
- **Water (VI1, MG1):**
  - The Open Blue is clear deep blue and the Ruins are clearer.
  - The map-edge haze ramp is 240 studs (was 380), so Megalodon fights at 1,000–1,440 out aren't inside it.
  - The fight light thins the water by 20%.
- **Stand-in (SK4b):** a skin still loading shows the fish flat in the shade's own colour. The tinted texture could only darken, which made the Nibbler maroon under Lilac.
- **Silver:**
  - **Angler skin (v14.1):** a step darker, and only the lure glows.
  - **Silver prey:** a whiter cool silver body with a small light over its back, plus a staggered mirror flash every 1.25–2 s.
- **UI:**
  - Phone: the tutorial card's distance line sits under its header, and the idle stick ring rests lower.
  - Phone: the Daily gift tiles list one reward per line.
  - PC: the full map draws on its own layer above DAILY and SHOP.
  - Centre banners queue on the stage (`Ui.stageBanner`).
  - A tutorial step finished the moment it began shows as done for 2.5 s with a line saying what happened.
- **Trails (TR3):** Ink Cloud is brighter and thicker, and Storm's arcs are wider with a glow.
- **Other:** a luau-lsp type fix in `Input.hold`, and Studio's `Terrain?` warning in `DenDoor` is fixed.
