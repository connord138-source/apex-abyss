## 3c. The catch round (new)

What changed:
- **Rare fish come per player, not per fish.** The old per-fish odds over ~4,100 fish gave a hunter a Crystal every ~5 min, which matches Connor's 3 in 5 minutes. Now each player in the biomes has their own stream of rare fish. They appear 340–750 studs away, in the player's biome, on fish they can eat, and never within 320 studs of anyone.
- **Sim targets for a player chasing glints, in minutes per catch:**

  | Silver | Golden | Glowing | Crystal | Prism |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | ~1 | ~5 (Calm 11, Golden Hour 2) | ~25 | ~100 (Blood Tide ~50) | ~2.5 per Prism Tide |

- **Skin goals:** N of a rarity from each of 5 prey species unlocks that skin for **every fish**:

  | Silver | Golden | Glowing | Crystal | Prism |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 10 × 5 | 10 × 5 | 5 × 5 | 2 × 5 | 1 × 5 |

- **Others:** the Catch Log is now a logbook, Silver is a new rarity with its own skin, and there are 8 new prey.
