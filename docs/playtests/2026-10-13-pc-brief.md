## What's new, all on claude/world

### Dens v2 (Connor, 2026-10-13)

"The middle of the dens also looks pretty crude and it's just sticks with spheres on the table. The dens need to be larger and have much more depth so players can build in them and show them off. It's meant to be like the players house/base. We will have furniture, rugs, lamps, and tons of items that will be rolled in once these steps are done."

- **A house dug down.** The hub stands on rock that's solid to y −80, so a den grows down into it. There are five levels:
  - **Nook:** the main room, holds 15 things.
  - **Burrow**, 1,000 coral (was 2,500): the main room runs back to r 150, holds 30.
  - **Hall**, 12,000: a shaft in the Burrow's floor drops to a vaulted hall at y −30, under the plaza's ring. The Coral Garden moves to its middle. Holds 50.
  - **Grotto**, 45,000: the hall opens into wide wings and a deep back. Holds 75.
  - **Sanctum**, 150,000 (new): a second shaft to the deepest storey at y −62. Holds 100.
  - Den Expansion adds 25 at any level; its description in `Config/Monetization` now says so.
  - At the top level there are about 10,000 sq studs of floor over three storeys (a Nook had 384). The rooms are barrel vaults with alcoves in warm stone, 5 studs of rock inside the line to the next den.
- **Decks:** every storey has a flat flagstone floor to build on: tiles of mixed sizes with dark joints, sitting 1–2 studs over the rock.
  - Six floor styles in the den menu: Flagstone (free), Driftwood Planks 800, Sandstone Tiles 1,500, Pearl Marble 4,000, Reef Mosaic 6,000, Glowing Obsidian 12,000 (the den's glow shows in its joints).
- **Architecture:** stone curbs with a glowing lip round each shaft and a light where it comes out below; pillars with sconces down the big rooms; orb lamps hanging down the vaults; two lamps in the Burrow. All follow the den's glow.
- **Build mode.** In your own den, press **B**, or BUILD at the top of the den menu (E at your pool). Leaving the den, or B again, ends it.
  - You keep swimming and aim with the camera. A ghost of the picked thing shows where it would go, outlined **green**, or **red** with the reason on the tray ("That's not on your floor", "Aim at a wall", "Your den holds 15 things").
  - Things go on decks, walls or ceilings, whichever they're made for, and can stand on each other (a lamp on a table, a table on a rug).
  - With nothing picked, aim at a placed thing (it outlines gold) to pick it up and move it, or put it back in the tray.
  - Keys: LEFT CLICK place / pick up, RIGHT CLICK drop what you hold, R / F turn 15° (hold to keep turning), Q / E previous / next thing in the tray, X (or Delete) put away, B done. Alt frees the cursor to click the tray's tiles.
  - Pad: RT place / pick up, LT drop, LB / RB turn, D-pad left / right pick, X put away, B done. Y, D-pad up / down and G do nothing meanwhile.
  - Phone: drag to aim (the ghost sits under the screen's middle), tap a tile in the tray, then PLACE, TURN (45°), PUT AWAY, DROP, DONE.
  - Bite and dash don't fire while building.
  - The tray along the bottom lists what you own and haven't placed (×count and where it goes); the header counts placed / cap.
  - Things bought in the den menu (now a THINGS section) go into the tray; you can own several of one.
  - The server checks every request again (it refuses anything outside the den, off a deck, past the cap or not in the tray) and saves it.
- **Old saves:** things in the old slots are placed where those slots were (or on the nearest free spot of the same floor), and things bought before become one copy each in the tray. This runs once per save.
- **New things:** Woven Kelp Rug (flat, others stand on it) and Jellyfish Lamp (hangs from the ceiling). That makes 16; the rolled furniture, rugs and lamps come next round.
- **The Coral Garden** (the "sticks with spheres on the table"):
  - In a Nook or Burrow it's a stone planter along the main room's back wall, under the trophy plaques, with a glowing seam on its front lip.
  - From the Hall on it's an oval bed sunk into the Hall's floor, ringed with rounded stones over a glow, with a couple of boulders.
  - Its corals (staghorn, fan, brain, tube sponges, anemones) are the imported reef props (StaghornCoral, FanCoral, BrainCoral, TubeSponge, Anemone), tinted. Where one isn't imported, a part-built coral of the same kind stands in (branching staghorn, not sticks with balls).
  - They grow one after another as it fills, and it sparkles and lights up when full.
  - A Sanctum grows 380 coral and 16 shells an hour.
- **Cave fix:** the rock under the cavern floor now goes all the way down to the shelf's hollow. The dome's carve had left a hidden void under the plaza (nobody could see it), and the new storeys would have opened into it.
- **Admin, for testing:** "Den: set the level (1-5)" and "Den: things into the Build tray" (N of every thing).
- The safe zone, the minimap's YOUR DEN, the Coral Garden's "resting in your den" and the camera all count the whole house.

### The 2026-10-12 report's fixes

- **Tab no longer frees the cursor** (in a game it opened Roblox's player list). Alt alone does; the CURSOR FREE chip says so.
- **Parts fit slender fish.** Rolled parts were sized for the round graybox body, so on the Moray Eel the Sail Fin and Veil Tail were huge ovals touching the back at one point.
  - Now each mesh fish has a part scale (Eel 0.55, 'Cuda 0.65, Reef Shark 0.8) and heights measured from its rigged model (the snout's middle, the back's top).
  - A sail stands about a third deep in the back, and back pieces ride the spine bone under them.
- **Hammerhead:** a bridge round the snout and two thick lobes swept back, with dark eyes at their tips, centred on the snout's height. It used to be a flat plate on top.
- **The full map in the hub** says YOUR DEN · THE HUB · safe (or A DEN, or THE HUB) instead of OPEN WATER.
