- **Shade cards are empty discs for ~1–1.2 s** after the roll starts, then fill in textured mid-spin.
  - Textured shades seen: Mandarin, Prismatic, Leopard, Ocean, and Moray Sunset, Rust and Aurora.
  - One white Prismatic frame at 1.0 s.

- **FPS:** 59.6 average, worst frame 47 ms.

### Why the discs stay empty (checked in a fresh Play session)

- **`CardPhoto.warm()` fetches nothing.** `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync({ folder })` on `ShadeSkins.Nibbler` returns in 0.00 s, and every skin's `GetAssetFetchStatus(ColorMapContent.Uri)` stays **None** (shot 37).
  - So `loaded()` never sees Success, and every shade card waits the full `WAIT_SKIN` (1.2 s). That matches the ~1.2 s of empty disc.
- **Preloading the URIs themselves does fetch them.** `PreloadAsync` with the 33 id strings went to Loading, then **Success 33**; Cuda, which wasn't preloaded, stayed None 33 (shot 38).
- **I tried that as a fix and reverted it.**
  - With the ids preloaded, the gate opened at once, but some cards then drew **white fish** for 0.3–0.5 s:
    - X-Ray Shade, which should be dark navy, was white at 0.3 s (shot 40).
    - Leopard and Seafoam were white at 0.45 s (shot 39).
    - Nebula was textured by 0.85 s (shot 41).
  - A fetch status of Success doesn't mean the ViewportFrame has drawn the texture yet.
  - White fish were the original complaint, so I didn't commit it. This needs a different "ready" signal.
