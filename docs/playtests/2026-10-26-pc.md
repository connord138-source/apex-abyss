### PJ1: prey look

## Notes for the PC session

- **The Dev Console can't open with the cursor locked:**
  - **Escape fails here:** pressing it closes the Dev Console.
  - **The workaround:** with the game focused, press M (the map), then Escape twice (Roblox's menu
    open and shut). The cursor is then free. Hide the map with
    `PlayerGui.MapWindow.Enabled = false`, and F9's profiler can be clicked with the rest of the HUD
    showing.
- **Clicks while the cursor is locked:** with Roblox's menu open, a click still warps to the
  screen's centre and misses. Use Escape to close it.

## Recommendations

- **Reef stepping:** work out a fish's move rate from how fast it's really drawn, not its
  configured speed. Either use its last step against the time since, or use the school's path speed
  with its sway.
  - **Who it's for:** the Butterflyfish (18.8 studs/s on screen against `speed` 5), Lionfish and
    Opaleye.
  - **A blunter fix:** halve `MOVE_STEP` for prey whose sway is large.
- **SchoolController:** next is `step`'s own per-fish body (35–42 ms/s at the nave and the ribcage).
  The moves (BulkMoveTo) have fallen to 22–42 ms/s.
