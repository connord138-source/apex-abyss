## FX2 follow-up I tried and reverted

`Cosmetics` `aura()` falls back to "glints" whenever a shade has a `sparkle` colour. As a result, the 13 effect skins that carry one still shed a ShadeAura on top of their effects: Diamond, Magma, Glacier, Divine, Aurora, Thunder, Exotic, Prismatic, Nebula, Ghost, Abyss Ink, Sunken Gold and Drowned Pearl. `Config/Shades` clears `aura` for them ("its effects take the aura's place").

Skipping the fallback for a shade with `fx` worked: Magma then had only `Fxembers/18 Fxdrips/8`. I reverted it, because it would change 13 skins I had just judged in FX2 with those glints on. Nothing was committed.
