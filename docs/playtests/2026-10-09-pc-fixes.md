## R3 Roll cards

### As merged: FAIL

- I watched each shade card's photo `ImageTransparency` every 0.05 s during a roll. That measurement doesn't depend on screenshots.
- **First roll of a fresh session, 5+ s after spawning:** every shade card was held at 0.985 for ~0.7 s and showed as an empty disc (shots 05, 06).
  - Examples: Bumblebee 0.37 → 1.05 s, Aurora 0.99 → 1.44 s, Divine 1.05 → 1.77 s.
  - Later rolls were fine, because by then each skin had been on a card once.
- **The warm-up itself works.** After a species switch, "CardWarmup" appears at 2.18 s (160×120, transparency 0.985, 33 fish, the right fish's skins) and goes at 4.92 s. Yet 14 of 14 Cuda shade cards were still held on the next roll.

### Cause

- With temporary debug prints (reverted), the warm-up reported **0 of 64 textures loaded** right after `PreloadAsync`, for both Nibbler and Cuda.
- A direct test on Puffer's 132 textures: `PreloadAsync` returned after 0.08 s with no error, all still `Loading`; 2 s later all were `Success`.
- `ready()` only stamps `fetchedAt` on its own first call, and the warm-up never called it after the downloads finished. So each skin's first card stamped it and waited `DRAW_DELAY`.

### Fixed: `e5b291b`, `CardPhoto.warm`

- While the warm-up viewport is up, it calls `ready(uri)` on its textures every 0.1 s, stamping each the moment it downloads.
- It stays up until all have loaded and been drawn for `DRAW_DELAY` + 0.3 s, at most 12 s.

### After the fix

- **Fresh session, first roll:** 17 shade cards, 0 held. Leopard, Ocean, Moss, Seafoam and Tiger were textured in the 0.15 s and 0.45 s frames (shots 07, 08).
