## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Linear growth:** every full Haul banks 5 levels (the tutorial's takes LV 1 → 6). Length grows by about the same each level (~0.14 ft a level, ~0.7 ft a load). LV 100 takes ~2.1 h of active play.
- **A fuller sea:** ~8,560 prey fish (was ~3,660), in more and bigger schools, most of all in the deep biomes. Respawns are about a third as long, and a fish coming back swims in from behind its school.
- **Bite feedback:** a snap sound, bubbles and a camera kick on every bite, a crunch when it lands, and a jaws marker at the screen's middle.
- **Leaving a den** waits until the plaza has loaded (SWIMMING OUT TO THE HUB · N%).
- **The den's way out** is a real doorway: an alcove in the front wall in the den's own doorway style, TO THE HUB on its nameplate. The den's title board moved over the trophy wall.
- **Tutorial:** a bigger card with a title per step and a centre banner for each new step.
- **Wardrobe:** cards run Common → Mythic; a locked skin shows as a dark silhouette, its name and how to get it still shown.
- **Window skins:** Nebula and ~17 other skins carry a pattern that holds still on screen while the fish swims through it (like Hatch & Snatch's Galaxy).
- **Sea-life companions:**
  - **Turtle:** swim beside one for 2 s and it joins you for 90 s; bites on you do ×0.8.
  - **Manta:** riding in its wake gives ×1.3 speed.
  - **Stingray:** skim low over one resting on the sand and it bursts away, stirring up 2 shells.
- **Vents:** no more placeholder spheres. The stains, flanges, crusts, pools, flowstone, shells, worm plumes and vent throats are all redesigned.
- **Reef-fish clouds** are small copies of the reef's own prey, not ellipsoids.
