## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Linear growth:** every full Haul banks 5 levels (the tutorial's takes LV 1 → 6). Length grows by about the same each level (~0.14 ft a level, ~0.7 ft a load). The sim puts LV 100 at ~2.1 h of active play.
- **A fuller sea:**
  - ~8,560 prey fish (was ~3,660), in more and bigger schools, most of all in the deep biomes.
  - Respawns are about a third as long, and a fish coming back swims in from behind its school.
  - Far spines wave every other frame.
  - The server's school lift yields, so its start line comes a few seconds after the others.
- **Bite feedback:** every bite has a snap sound, bubbles, a camera kick and a jaws marker at the screen's middle. A landed bite adds a crunch and a red ring.
- **Leaving a den** waits until the plaza has loaded (SWIMMING OUT TO THE HUB · N%).
- **The den's way out** is a real doorway: an alcove in the front wall, in the den's own doorway style, with TO THE HUB on its nameplate. The den's title board moved over the trophy wall.
- **Tutorial:** a bigger card with a title per step, and a centre banner for each new step.
- **Wardrobe:** cards run Common → Mythic. A locked skin is a dark silhouette, but its name and how to get it still show.
- **Window skins:** Nebula, Void, Comet, Starfall, Diamond, Glacier, Divine, Aurora, Abyss Eye, Prismatic, Exotic, Chrome, Thunder, Magma, Coral Bloom, Jade, Sunburst and Lantern King carry a pattern that holds still on screen while the fish swims through it (like Hatch & Snatch's Galaxy).
- **Sea-life companions:**
  - **Turtle:** swim beside one for 2 s and it joins you for 90 s at your side; bites on you do ×0.8. It won't join again for 4 min.
  - **Manta:** riding in its wake gives ×1.3 speed.
  - **Stingray:** skim low over one resting on the sand and it bursts away, stirring up 2 shells (one every 90 s).
- **Vents:** no more placeholder spheres. The stains, flanges, crusts, pools, flowstone, shells, worm plumes and every vent throat are redesigned.
- **Reef-fish clouds** are small copies of the reef's own prey, not ellipsoids.

**PV — prey volume and cost.**
1. Swim 30 s each in the Kelp, Reef, Open Blue, Ruins, Vents and Trench (admin teleport).

   - `Workspace.Prey` attributes `Detail` / `Built` / `Drawn`;

**RS — respawn.**
1. In the Kelp, eat most of a school.
2. Its fish should swim back in from behind within ~3–5 s, never popping in where they were.
3. Swim straight back into a returning fish: it shouldn't vanish and come back (a rejected eat).

**BF — bite feedback.**
1. Right-click into open water: you should get the snap sound, bubbles, a small camera kick and the fangs closing at the screen's middle.
2. Bite a predator (and a fish your size if you have one): the crunch and a red ring should burst.
