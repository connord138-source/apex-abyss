### D8 Deep bioluminescence
- **Open Blue, deep floor (796 ft): PASS.** Faint blue specks around you, a faint sparkle wake after a dash, and small glowing patches. `PlanktonMats` has 215 children; the brief says 92 patches, so a patch is probably several parts.
- **Trench bottom (y −910): FAIL.** With and without a lamp it's **pure black**: no teal lift and no visible specks, patches or glow specks. Even Magma and Lanternfish are invisible.
- **Measured without a lamp:**
  - Exposure −3.63
  - OceanGrade brightness −0.62, tint ≈(0.21, 0.30, 0.37), saturation −0.42
  - Atmosphere density **1.00**
  - The plankton emitters do run: `Camera.Plankton` at 44.8/s and 24.1/s, teal, LightEmission 1
- **With the Deep Lantern:**
  - Exposure −2.42, brightness −0.34, density **1.00**, haze 2.8.
  - The lamp itself is fine: PointLight range 90, brightness 2.2.
  - The camera is 22.8 studs from the fish, so at density 1.0 the fish is fogged out before the grade even applies.
- **Verdict:** **far too strong**. Not "very very faint", but invisible. Likely fix (cloud session): cap the Trench grade's Atmosphere density well below 1 near the camera (lower again when lit), and ease the colour-correction brightness. Then the lamp, the glow shades and the plankton can read.
- **FPS at the Trench bottom:** 60.0 average, worst frame 18 ms. On the first load in the hub: 59.8, worst 42 ms, 60,440 Workspace descendants.
