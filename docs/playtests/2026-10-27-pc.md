## Notes for the PC session

- **Never toggle the map while `MapWindow` is disabled.** It's then open but unseen, and pressing M
  again closes it. Once the cursor relocked I couldn't get it back that way, and I restarted Play.
- **Getting the command bar back while locked:** left-click the game view (a middle-click didn't
  focus it) and press Escape (Roblox's menu). Then click the command bar twice: with the menu open,
  that click lands.
- **The clip readings lag:** `GetClipCursor` read "locked" with Roblox's menu open until the next
  click.
- **Switching species:** `Remotes.SelectSpecies:FireServer(id)` from your den, then the admin's
  `setLevel`.

## Recommendations

- **Use the jump budget:** held fish now jump about 1 px, under half the ~2.3 px `MOVE_STEP`
  allows, so they're moved more often than smoothness needs.
  - **Cause:** the pace's turn term (`turned × length × 0.5`) and its peak that settles over ~0.5 s
    overestimate.
  - **A fix:** halve the turn term, or settle faster (`PACE_SETTLE` 4), or raise `MOVE_STEP` toward
    0.0045.
  - **Expected effect:** the median jump near 2 px, and SchoolController back toward last round's
    66–121 ms/s (`BulkMoveTo` is what grew).
- **Glow lights:** keep a light on its fish until another is clearly nearer (a few studs of
  hysteresis). Or rank a held fish by its current distance, as before.
- **Pinnacle:** thin the raft fish's tiers (they move every frame within 70 studs and every other
  frame to 120), or use a static Sardine mesh instead of the rigged model, to bring
  LandmarkController back under ~15.
