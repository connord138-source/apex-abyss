## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Private den interiors.** Connor: "Dens also still need to be substantially bigger". He chose private interiors.
  - Each den is now its own block of rock far outside the play area, built at the hub's height. The ring of twelve round the plaza no longer limits its size.
  - On the plaza, each doorway is a shallow alcove with a **bubble portal** at its back. Swim into it: the screen washes over with bubbles, and you arrive **over your Haul Pool** inside, so swimming home banks your Haul.
  - The way out is a portal on the den's front wall. It puts you back on the plaza, in front of your doorway, facing the plaza.
  - The rooms are about 4× bigger:
    - The Nook is 60 × 60 and 39 studs tall.
    - A full Sanctum has about 46,000 square studs of deck over three storeys.
    - Rooms have upright walls under a shallow arched ceiling.
    - Capacities are 20 / 40 / 70 / 110 / 160 things.
  - Anyone can visit a den that someone lives in. An empty den's portal stays shut and says "No one lives in this den yet". When an owner leaves the game, any visitors are put back on the plaza.
  - Inside a den you count as being in the hub:
    - It's safe, with no pressure.
    - Lighting, sound and music are the hub's.
    - The minimap shows you at your doorway with YOUR DEN.
  - The tutorial's trail leads through your doorway, then to the pool.
  - **Old saves:** things placed on the old lower storeys move down with their decks once. Anything that no longer fits goes back to the tray.
- **Toon materials.** Connor: walls "look too much like garrymod textures or semi realistic". Every stock material the world uses gets a flat, cel-shaded tile game-wide, on Terrain and parts alike.
  - The 11 materials are Rock, Slate, Basalt, Sandstone, Sand, Mud, CrackedLava, Pebble, Marble, WoodPlanks and Wood. The tiles are big plates, bands and planks with bold seams.
  - They're grey, so the world's own colours still tint them; CrackedLava has its own glowing cracks.
  - All 11 passed the classifier screen in the cloud, highest 0.098.
  - They need uploading (step 2). Until then everything stays stock.

## 1. Sync

1. Merge `origin/claude/world` into `claude/core-systems`.
2. Run `rokit install`, then `rojo serve`, and connect the plugin.
3. Format with `stylua --line-endings Windows src --glob "!**/Packages/**"`.
4. Play once. The Output should show no errors.
   - Note how long it takes from pressing Play until your fish appears. The server now carves your den on join.

## 2. Toon materials

1. **Before** anything else, take screenshots of the current look: the hub cave wall, the plaza, Kelp sand, Reef rock, a Vents basin, a den floor and a vendor stall (T0).
2. Screen the tiles (nothing is uploaded by this; classifier on):


   It must exit 0. If it doesn't, stop and report the output.
3. Upload them in two batches a few minutes apart (Asset Manager → Import, as Images, Start Import): first Rock, Slate, Basalt, Sandstone, Sand and Mud; then CrackedLava, Pebble, Marble, WoodPlanks and Wood.
4. Right-click each row in the Import Queue → Copy AssetId, and paste each id into `src/shared/Config/Materials.luau` in place of that material's `id = 0`. Commit it as "Toon material ids" and push.
5. With Rojo synced, stop play. In **edit mode**, paste `tools/studio/make_toon_materials.luau` into the Command Bar and Run. It should print `[make_toon_materials] 11 toon materials on, 0 still stock (id 0). Now File → Save.`
6. Save (Ctrl+S).
