## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Private den interiors.** Connor: "Dens also still need to be substantially bigger". He chose private interiors.
  - Each den is now its own block of rock far outside the play area, built at the hub's height. The ring of twelve round the plaza no longer limits its size.
  - On the plaza, each doorway is a shallow alcove with a **bubble portal** at its back. Swim into it: the screen washes over with bubbles, and you arrive **over your Haul Pool** inside, so swimming home banks your Haul.
  - The way out is a portal on the den's front wall. It puts you back on the plaza, in front of your doorway, facing the plaza.
  - The rooms are about 4× bigger:
    - The Nook is 60 × 60 and 39 studs tall.
    - A full Sanctum has about 46,000 square studs of deck over three storeys.
    - Rooms have upright walls under a shallow arched ceiling.
    - Capacities are 20 / 40 / 70 / 110 / 160 things.
  - Anyone can visit a den that someone lives in. An empty den's portal stays shut and says "No one lives in this den yet". When an owner leaves the game, any visitors are put back on the plaza.
  - Inside a den you count as being in the hub:
    - It's safe, with no pressure.
    - Lighting, sound and music are the hub's.
    - The minimap shows you at your doorway with YOUR DEN.
  - The tutorial's trail leads through your doorway, then to the pool.
  - **Old saves:** things placed on the old lower storeys move down with their decks once. Anything that no longer fits goes back to the tray.
- **Toon materials.** Connor: walls "look too much like garrymod textures or semi realistic". Every stock material the world uses gets a flat, cel-shaded tile game-wide, on Terrain and parts alike.
  - The 11 materials are Rock, Slate, Basalt, Sandstone, Sand, Mud, CrackedLava, Pebble, Marble, WoodPlanks and Wood. The tiles are big plates, bands and planks with bold seams.
  - They're grey, so the world's own colours still tint them; CrackedLava has its own glowing cracks.
  - All 11 passed the classifier screen in the cloud, highest 0.098.
  - They need uploading (step 2). Until then everything stays stock.
- **Your round-1 findings** (2026-10-16-pc.md, "For a decision"). Connor approved every recommendation:
  - **Pad, pass track:** every tile is selectable. The D-pad walks along the track and the strip scrolls to follow the selected tier.
  - **Pad, LOG:** the Shop has a **Log** row (OPEN) beside the Tide Pass row.
  - **Claiming a tile:** a click, tap or A on a READY tile claims it. Tiers are claimed in order, so everything unlocked before it comes too. On the locked Deep row, the tile opens the Deep Pass purchase. The Deep card keeps UNLOCKED (not OPEN).
  - **Phone Build mode:**
    - The editor is a slim full-width bar along the bottom. Its tray folds away with **TRAY ▼ / ▲**, and folds by itself once you pick a thing.
    - The help line sits at the top.
    - The swimming HUD is hidden while you edit: the stick, BITE, DASH, ITEMS, HEAL, the Haul card, the HUD buttons, the wallet and the minimap. DONE brings it back.
  - **Aim on the viewed storey only** (every device): a tap or click down a shaft's mouth no longer places on the deck below.
  - **Turning beats snapping:** after R / F (LB / RB, TURN), snapping still backs a piece onto the wall but keeps your turn. A fresh pick still faces out.
  - **Stacks settle:** when the thing under a stacked piece is put away or moved, the piece drops onto whatever is below it, or the deck. If there's nowhere it can stand, it goes back to the tray with a note.
  - **Second Chance** has its own photo: a red-and-white life ring.
  - **Phone toasts:** while a menu window or the Build bar is up, toasts show as a thin strip under the top bar.
  - **Whale Fall:** every carcass now has the faint outline (it was deep floors only), and a plume of chum rises 40–90 studs above it, over the kelp and the reef.
  - **Skins that read dark, flat or muddy:**
    - Their moving layers are stronger: CatchGolden, CudaChrome, ShkScarred, MorRibbon, WeekWreck, WeekWave and PassAbyssalCurrent.
    - They're also retextured in a small patch pack, **skins v10** (step 2b).
    - The 'Cuda's Thunder no longer paints lightning over the teeth.
  - Unchanged on purpose: a pad still enters Build from the den menu (B means back on a console). Panning out of the den door can't happen now that dens are private interiors.

4. Play once. The Output should show no errors.
   - Note how long it takes from pressing Play until your fish appears. The server now carves your den on join.

6. Save (Ctrl+S).

## 3. Checks

Play, open the admin panel, and keep the Output open.

- **D1 Enter and bank.**
  - Spawn: you appear in your den, over the pool.
  - Swim out through the exit portal on the front wall to reach the plaza, then back in through your doorway's portal. You should see a bubble wash, then arrive over the pool.
  - With a Haul aboard (catch something first), going in banks it.
  - Check the Output is clean.
- **D2 Size and look.** Admin-set your level to 100 and look round the Nook.
  - Does it feel big for a full-size fish?
  - The walls stand upright under an arched ceiling.
  - The lights are enough (not dim), and there are no holes in the rock.
  - Take screenshots from the entrance and from the back.
- **D3 Levels.** Admin "Den: set the level" to 2, 3, 4 and 5 in turn. At each level:
  - The den re-carves.
  - Each shaft is open; swim down it, and the shaft lift works.
  - The garden moves to the Hall's bed at level 3.
  - Pillars and lamps are in place, with no rock poking through the floors or walls.
  - Take one screenshot per level and one of each storey at level 5.
- **D4 Exit.** The way-out portal puts you on the plaza in front of your doorway, facing the plaza, and doesn't throw you straight back in.
- **D5 Visitors** (2 clients):

  - A leaves the game: B is put back on the plaza.

- **D6 Build mode in a big den.**
  - The view zooms out far enough to see a whole room (up to 120).
