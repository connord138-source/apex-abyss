## The white glow (owner: "only certain skins")

The cause was my own change in v53, plus the way lights work:

1. **Ceiling lamps too strong.** 384e8d6 raised them to range 60 and brightness 1.15 / 0.95, so a
   fish over the pool took about 9 at once. Lamps off: the plain Nibbler is true orange.
2. **Lights pass through rock.** From den level 3 the lower storeys' lamps, 26 studs under the
   Nook's floor, lit the fish from below: 20 lamps instead of 11, and nearer than its own.
3. **The pool's cyan light** right under the fish turned over-bright orange white.
4. **Pale skins have no headroom,** which is why only some showed it. Dark and saturated skins
   (Maroon, Glowing, Riptide) held up. Drowned Pearl, Silver, Diamond, Divine, Prismatic, Lunar
   and Glacier went to or near flat white.

The glow map isn't the cause: turning it off changed little. Darkening the colour map did.

**Fix:** lamps 0.55 / 0.45, less under a lower roof (`LAMP_FULL_ROOF`); pool light 0.5; a client
`DenLampController` shows only the lamps of the storey the camera is on, plus those by a shaft;
`Config/Shades` `TONES` multiply the eight palest skins (0.72–0.85).

Checked: all 29 skins of all 6 fish at the pool under the softer lamps, before the tones were
added; then the 29 Nibbler skins again with everything in. Tones were compared on and off in the
Open Blue, where they make no visible difference. On the plaza and in the Reef the only shots
were from the fish's shadow side, so they say little. Kelp shots were blocked by kelp. Toned skins
on the other five fish, and Lunar and Glacier at all, weren't looked at again with the tone in.

**Bug caught in this round:** the controller first kept each lamp's brightness in a weak table.
Roblox drops an Instance key from one while the lamp still exists, so lamps came back at 0 and
the Sanctum went black. It now reads a server attribute, `Full`.

Still flat: Golden reads bright yellow with little pattern. A skin worn for the first time in a
session shows its plain stand-in for a few seconds, white on the pale ones. That's the existing
loader, unchanged.
