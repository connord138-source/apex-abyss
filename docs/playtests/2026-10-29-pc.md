## Notes for the PC session

- **Measure with the tide in mind:** a Sardine Run changes the Kelp's near fish and every spot's built
  count. Use admin `forceTide me Calm` (or `Sardine`) to compare like for like, and `forceTide me ""`
  to put tides back on the clock.

## Recommendations

- **Near bait-ball fish:** don't hold a fish within 40 studs when it's in a tide school, or when its
  pace is over ~10 studs/s. The cheapest fix is `MOVE_NEAR` 40 for `school.tide` schools only, and
  the rest of this round's savings stay. Then re-check the 25–60 band on a forced Sardine Run.
- **Pinnacle:** LandmarkController is 14.2. If it should keep its margin under 15, cap the raft fish
  within 70 studs at every 2nd frame, since their jumps there are under 3 px.
