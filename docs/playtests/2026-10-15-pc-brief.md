## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Whale Fall model** (Connor: the part-built whale "looks very rough").
  - New bundle `whalefall_glb` (sha256 starts `d3ac4436`) with one file, `props/WhaleFall.glb`.
  - It's a chunky low-poly humpback resting on the floor: eye shut, barnacles, and two bites showing bone-white ribs over a dark hollow. No blood.
  - It's already cleaned in the cloud: normal map stripped, colours made safe, and both images (colour and ORM) cleared by the classifier screen (0.000 and 0.046).
  - `ChumController.carcass` fits it to 60 studs long and turns it onto X. The part-built whale stays as the fallback.
  - `organize_imports.luau` files it into `WorldProps`.
- **Bait cap** (your decision 1). A bait now gives at most **3 levels' worth** for the fish that swallows it (`Config/Hooks` `baitLevels`).
  - Expected for a LV 21 fish: about 2.8 kg, or about 5.6 kg with ×2 Mass, from any biome's boat. It was 4.6 t from a Trench boat.
  - Fish at the biome's level are unchanged. A LV 45 fish still gets about +46 kg with ×2 Mass from an Open Blue bait.
- **Sheen keeps off the teeth** (your decision 2).
  - A moving sheen can't be masked, so on a toothed fish (Angler, 'Cuda, Moray, Reef Shark) it now leaves out the face the head points out of.
  - From the front, the teeth stay bone white. The flanks, back and belly still shimmer.
- **Screen base on the PC** (your decision 3).
  - `screen_shades.py` now reads each fish's own texture out of `assets/fbx/species/<Fish>.fbx` by itself. Those are the same pixels as the cloud's `.npz` bases.
  - So the plain command works with no `--base`, and the strict fallback no longer applies to the six fish.
- **Wheel chip.** While your own win is still showing and nobody is waiting, the chip reads **SPIN AGAIN (n)**, not "JOIN THE LINE".

## 2. Fetch and import the whale

1. Run `python tools/fetch_assets.py`.
   - It should print `fetched whalefall_glb: 1 file(s) -> assets/glb (sha256 d3ac4436)`.
   - If it refuses the bundle over a sha256 mismatch, stop and report it.
2. In `ApexAbyss.rbxl`, File → Import 3D **only** `assets/glb/props/WhaleFall.glb`.
3. Paste `tools/studio/organize_imports.luau` into the Command Bar and Run.
4. Confirm `ReplicatedStorage.WorldProps.WhaleFall` exists, then Save (Ctrl+S).
