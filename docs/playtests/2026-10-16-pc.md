## The fixes

**672aa76, BD2.** `BuildController` `clampView` clamped the focus to the box round all of a storey's rooms. In the Sanctum that box's corners lie in rock between the wings and the back. From there the camera's ray out of the rock started inside it and hit nothing, so the view went into the hub and out to sea. Now the focus goes to the nearest point inside one of the rooms (`DenSpace.rooms`), `VIEW_INSET` 3 studs off the walls. `storeyBox` is gone.

**f56cc73, PS1 (controller).** The Shop lists a **Tide Pass** row after the Pod row (OPEN → `MenuController.openPass`), gold with "N to claim" when rewards wait, like the Pod row that's there for the same reason.

**348479e, PS1 (phone).** `openPass` `attachVisible` converts the view to canvas offsets: `canvasScale()` = `AbsoluteCanvasSize.X / CanvasSize.X.Offset`. It's checked again when `AbsoluteCanvasSize` changes, since the canvas's screen size settles a frame later. The opening scroll to the current tier uses the same scale. On PC the scale is 1, so nothing changes there.

All three: StyLua `--line-endings Windows --check` clean, and selene shows the same 3 warnings before and after (shadowing, already there).

## For a decision (not small fixes, not made)

1. **Pad: the pass track can't scroll.** The tiles and the `Tiers` strip are `Selectable = false`. Selection never enters the track: D-pad down from the header goes nowhere, and the right stick does nothing. A pad sees tiers 1–7 only. CLAIM ALL still claims everything unlocked. A fix would make the tiles (or the strip) selectable so the frame scrolls to the selected tier.
2. **Pad: LOG has no route either.** LOG is a HUD button like PASS was (`Selectable = false`), and the Shop has no row for it, so a controller can't open the Catch Log, Bounties, Weekly or Tides. A Shop row like the new Tide Pass one would do.
3. **Phone Build mode is cramped.**
   - The editor panel (680 × 172) sits in the middle third of the phone screen. With the HUD row above it and the Haul card below it, little of the floor is left to tap. A placed Coral Lamp stayed entirely behind the panel until I orbited it out.
   - The swim stick, BITE, DASH, ITEMS and HEAL stay on screen in the editor.
   - From the Hall's view, a tap on the Sanctum shaft's opening placed a Coral Lamp on the **Sanctum** deck below (y −66), not the storey being viewed.
4. **'Cuda Thunder paints lightning over the teeth** (SK2). This is in the skin texture, so the fix is in the Thunder recipe or its glow map (the teeth texels, `teeth.json`). It needs a re-pack and re-screen of the Cuda GLB, which is the cloud's job.
5. **Earned gold reads olive or brown on dark-lit fish:** CatchGolden on the Nibbler, 'Cuda, Puffer and Moray (fine on the Angler). Several Week and Pass skins read dark and flat from the side (WeekWreck, PassAbyssalCurrent), as do MorRibbon, CudaChrome and ShkScarred.
6. **Smaller:**
   - The Deep card reads **UNLOCKED** where the brief says OPEN.
   - Pass tiles don't claim by click, only CLAIM ALL does.
   - Second Chance uses the Lucky Charm's photo.
   - Pad Build mode is entered only from the den menu; pad B only ends it.
   - Panning on a pad can take the camera just outside den 1's door into the cavern (not into rock).
   - A stacked Shell Pile floats ~1.1 studs after its base is put away.
   - Snapping overrides R / F next to walls.
   - On the phone, toasts draw over open panels.
   - A Whale Fall in the kelp forest is hard to see from any distance.
