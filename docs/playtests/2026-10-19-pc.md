**Bug 1, fixed (4dde9ea).**
- After the bank in the den, the fish stayed frozen: two exits went `go out: wash false`.

- `Input.hold(owner, on)` now keeps one hold per owner (bank, den, item wheel, start screen), and `locked` is on while any is held.
- Re-test: a bank on arrival, then `go out: wash true`.

**Bug 2, fixed (aafbf13): phone keys.**
- In the Device Simulator (iPhone 13), step 1's card showed W A S D, MOUSE, SPACE / CTRL and "Tap ALT to free the cursor" for the whole step, while the rest of the HUD was in its touch layout (`TU1-01-phone-card-keyboard-keys`).

- The card now redraws its caps and tip when the device kind changes.
