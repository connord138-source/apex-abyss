It covers a new Haul pace, the cliff as a reef wall, the Vents field, sea life models and edible crabs, the start screen's rolling shots, and predators with jaws that hunt like predators. **Don't publish:** Connor publishes from the saved file.

## What changed (cloud, `claude/world`)

- **Haul pace (Connor: "A full haul should take 15-20 fish first time and hold more each time allowing them to level up faster and faster"):**
  - **A full Haul is food:** it holds a set kg of fish eaten and banks a set number of levels.
  - **Display:** the Haul card, pops, the tutorial count and every toast show kg of food. A 0.4 kg Garibaldi reads "+0.4 kg".
  - **Full Haul by level:** 4.4 kg at LV 1, 5.2 kg at LV 8, 85 kg at LV 25, 1.5 t at LV 55, 5 t at LV 100.
  - **Levels per bank:** the tutorial's Haul takes LV 1 → 8. After that a full Haul banks 1 level at LV 8, rising to 2 at LV 100.
  - **Sim:** LV 100 in ~4.1 h of active play (was ~22 h).

    | Level | 15 | 25 | 40 | 55 | 70 | 85 | 100 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | Active play | 10 min | 23 min | 45 min | 1.2 h | 2.1 h | 3.2 h | 4.1 h |

    | Band | Tutorial | 8–15 | 15–25 | 25–40 | 40–55 | 55–70 | 70–85 | 85–100 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | Fish per full Haul | ~23 | ~24 | ~26 | ~55 | ~51 | ~96 | ~178 | ~205 |

  - **Double Haul tiers** moved to 1.2 / 63 kg of growth, so the price breaks stay at ~LV 22 and ~LV 58.
- **The smallest prey are longer:** Minnow ×1.25, Sardine ×1.15, Ghost Shrimp and Hatchetfish ×1.2, Lanternfish ×1.12, Garibaldi and Opaleye ×1.08. They are drawn, hit and swallowed that long; their kg and the schools' spread are unchanged.
- **The cliff is a reef wall:**
  - Strata bands, spurs, an undercut lip, crevices, caves, columns, ledges, a crest of reef heads, and talus.
  - Coral, fans, plates, whips and sponges dressed over it.
  - New props: `cliff_glb`.
  - A new map chart.
- **The Vents field:**
  - **The Forge:** a ~175-stud vent mountain with a crater lava lake, runnels, lava pools and an ash column, plus a shoulder cone.
  - Black-smoker complexes, hex basalt causeways, rimstone terraces, a brine pool, three tube-worm forests, mussel beds, and pillow lava between the basins.
- **Sea life:** real turtle, manta, stingray, humpback and crab models with proper motion (`ambient_fbx`), and part-built stand-ins until they're imported.
- **Edible crabs:** six kinds, from the 0.1 kg Shore Crab in the kelp to the 12 kg King Crab in the Trench. They scuttle sideways round their spots. Every crab you see is edible, and one too big for you raises its claws.
- **Start screen:** rolling shots of real schools in eight places, with a documentary caption, letterbox and murk or bubble cuts. The old animated scene is the loading layer and the fallback.
- **Predators:**
  - **Jaws:** the Megalodon, Reef Shark and 'Cuda have a jaw (re-rigged `boss_fbx` and `species_fbx`).
  - **Motion:** a gape through the tell, a snap with a head shake, burst-and-glide tail beats, a C-bend into turns, and bank.
  - **Hunting:** a miss comes round in ~2 s, never a slow pivot. The Megalodon's bite commits early, so a sideways dodge escapes.
  - **Size:** the Megalodon is 175 studs (was 130).
  - **Your own fish** now bends into its turns too.
- **Den Banner:** on every glow the cloth is a deeper, richer version of the glow colour, so the gold emblem and trim show on Gold and Violet no longer reads lilac.
- **Your two fixes are merged:** DP3 (the ceiling lamp) and PR7 (big fish's sway capped at their speed).

## 1. Sync

1. Merge `origin/claude/world` into `claude/core-systems`.
2. Run `rokit install`, then `rojo serve`, and connect the plugin.
3. Format with `stylua --line-endings Windows src --glob "!**/Packages/**"`.
4. Play once. The Output should show no errors. Copy these start lines word for word:
   - `[Cliff] …`
   - `[Vents] …`
   - `[Ruins] …`
   - `[ForageService] …` (with the crab count)
   - `[ForageService] … prey schools lifted …`

## 2. Imports (screen first; never upload a normal map)

Run `python tools/fetch_assets.py` once first. Screen every colour map with `python tools/shades/screen_shades.py <file>` before importing it; each must exit 0.

1. **Sea life** (`ambient_fbx`, sha256 58e7aef4…):
   - File → Import 3D the five files in `assets/fbx/ambient/` (Turtle, Manta, Stingray, Humpback, Crab), with rig and textures on.
   - Use Import All.
2. **Predator jaws** (`boss_fbx` 3477e8c2… and `species_fbx` a13009ba…):
   - File → Import 3D **only** `assets/fbx/bosses/Megalodon.fbx`, `assets/fbx/species/ReefShark.fbx` and `assets/fbx/species/Cuda.fbx`, with rig and textures on. Use Import All.
   - Don't re-import the Giant Squid or the other species.
   - Their colour maps are the already-accepted ones, and the mesh and UVs are unchanged, so the skins still fit.
3. **Cliff props** (`cliff_glb`, sha256 51e843ee…):
   - File → Import 3D `assets/glb/props/PlateCoral.glb`, `BarrelSponge.glb`, `SeaWhips.glb` and `LaceFan.glb`, no rig.
   - Rename any row called "Scene" to its file name.
4. Run `tools/studio/organize_imports.luau` once. It should print:
   - "rigged sea life" five times, and "SurfaceAppearance … made" for any model textured by TextureID
   - `Megalodon: rigged, Jaw, FinL, FinR`
   - `Cuda: rigged, Jaw`
   - `ReefShark: rigged, Jaw`

   It also refreshes `PreyModels.Barracuda` and `PreyModels.ReefShark`. Save the place.
5. **Map chart:** screen `assets/textures/map_chart.png`, upload it, and paste the new id into `Config/Textures` `mapChart`.
