### Swimming into boulders

- **Method:** the 10 nearest boulders to the Kelp Shallows spawn (5–15 studs across). The fish was pointed at each from 9 studs out, and I held W with the cursor on the rock. Every frame I logged:
  - the closest distance from the fish's root to the rock surface (a ray at the rock);
  - whatever the fish was stuck against whenever it stalled (a 0.8-stud blockcast ahead).
- **The fish's collider:** the root is a 0.8-stud cube, so touching shows as about 0.4.
- **Head-on contacts:** rock 1 from +X 0.37 and from the top 0.37; rock 2 from the top 0.28; rock 3 (jagged) from −X 0.15. The 0.15 means the box sat about 0.25 into the rock's outline at one corner of the hull.
- **No invisible walls:** whenever the fish stalled, the thing in front of it was the rock's own visible mesh, or another coral in the way. It never stalled against anything invisible.
- **A limit of the method:** many runs glanced off the rounded rocks, because the fish steers toward the cursor and slides along the surface. Those runs ended 1–9 studs to the side; they're not "stopped short".
- **No food under a rock:** of the 1,336 food items within 520 of the hub, 0 have a boulder over them (a ray straight up) and 0 sit in a coral's core.
- **Screenshot:** `01`, a boulder on the sand near the hub with the fish against it and shrimp on the floor.

## F3 Kelp — PASS

- **Lean:** a Giant Kelp in the Shallows, stalk 53.6 studs tall.
  - The current alone tilts it up to 4.8°.
  - With my fish held in the stalk at mid-height, it leans to 12.1°. Clearing the fish there needs about atan(4.5 / 26.8) ≈ 9.5° (the 3.5 give plus the fish's radius).
  - **Spring-back:** after the fish left, the tilt every 0.15 s read 6 6 4 4 4 6 5 5 1 1 1 4 4 4°, so it's back within the sway in about 0.3 s. That's faster than "about a second", but smooth.
- **Fade:** with the camera inside the stalk, the plant's mesh read LocalTransparencyModifier **0.75**; with the camera outside, **0.00**. No flicker seen.
- **FPS** in the forest (PC, HD 1080): 60.0 average (16.7 ms), worst frame 22.2 ms over 601 frames.
- **A second client seeing the lean:** PASS, in the pull-back round's 2-client session (`2026-10-10-pc-pullback.md`). Player1 was held 4 studs over the foot of an 80-stud Giant Kelp. Player2's view of the stalk read 0.2–4.4° of sway before, up to 20.4° while Player1 was in it, and normal sway again about 0.5 s after it left.
  - Held halfway up the same stalk (50 studs over the foot), the lean is only about 5°, inside the sway, as the formula says (the give over the height).
- **Screenshot:** `03`, the fish in the forest. The forest is dense, so a side view that shows the lean clearly kept being blocked by other plants; the numbers above are the evidence.

- **Note:** with the cursor free, a click on a HUD *panel* that isn't a button (I clicked the level card) counts as "the game" and locks it. With a window open it doesn't, because the menu wants the cursor. A small thing; panels could be made to swallow the click.
