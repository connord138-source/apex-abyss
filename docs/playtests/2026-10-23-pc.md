**How I swam:** the fish was moved 300 studs along the cathedral's axis at 14 studs/s with the

| Run | One-frame moves over 0.5 studs | Biggest |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 55 | 6.46 studs |
| 2 (after a respawn) | 55 | 4.83 studs |
| 3 (after a respawn) | 55 | 6.15 studs |
| 4 (fresh Play, clean) | 45 | 6.21 studs |

- **The same frame each time:** in the three runs I timed (2–4), every one of them happened in a
  single frame 11.0–11.5 s in, with the fish just past the nave's middle. Run 1 wasn't timed.
- **Which pieces:** tall stalk pieces 43–85 studs up, 65–97 studs from the camera and 100–127 from
  the fish, behind the camera.
- **What they do:** in run 4 each piece went from 3–6 studs off its rest pose to exactly 0.00 in
  that frame. That is the `settle` path (KelpController lines 378–384): plants the latest
  `rescan` didn't list are put straight back at rest, with no ease, though they are well inside
  `REACH`. Between the snaps nothing else jumps.
- **Likely cause (from reading the code, not confirmed):**
  - `filePlant` puts a plant in the grid by `GetPivot()` when the Model arrives. With streaming
    that can be before its parts, so its pivot may read as the origin.
  - `rescan` looks only at the cells in a square of camera ±200. The hub's cell drops out of it
    once the camera is ~280 studs from the hub.
  - Those plants would then be dropped even while in view. The snap comes when the camera is
    about there.
- **Shots:** `kc3_swim_nave`.
- **My mistake:** my own test pin once used `CFrame.lookAt(p, p)` (NaN), which deleted my fish's
  root on both sides. Runs 2 and 3 are from after a respawn, and run 4 is clean.

## Recommendations

- **Kelp snap:** file plants by their parts once they're in (or re-file on the first `collect`),
  and ease a settled plant to rest instead of setting it there.
- **LandmarkController at the pinnacle:** moving fewer parts is the only lever left.
  - Merge each part-built jelly into one MeshPart, or into fewer parts.
  - Or move the far jellies (200–500 studs) every eighth frame, or not at all past ~300.
- **Wolf Eels:** don't let an eel take a spot far over the floor as its hole, or over the drop-off
  at shelf height. The four high eels can't reach a fish, and #41 sits by shallow water.
- **Kelp buoy:** tag the chain with the float, or hang the top links from the float.
