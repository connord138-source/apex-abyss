## For a decision

1. **About 3 of a whale's 18 chunks sit inside the carcass.**
   - Seen: 3 of 18 in the Trench, about 5 in the Coral Reef.
   - Over 300 seeds of `Chum.offsets("whale", 18, 27, id * 7919)`, a whale has a mean of 2.99 inside (0–6).
   - Cause: the ring is a circle of radius 12–27 in world X/Z, but the carcass always lies along world X with a half-length of 30. Every chunk near angle 0 or π lands inside it.
   - Options:
     - Make the ring an ellipse that clears the body (e.g. 34–44 along X, 12–27 across).
     - Or push any offset that falls inside the body's ellipse out to its edge + 3 studs.
   - It's shared code, so the server's eat checks stay in step.
2. **In the Coral Reef the whale comes to rest with coral through it.**
   - `ChumService` `openFloor` samples only five columns: the centre and ±27 studs on X and Z.
   - The reef's tube sponges, staghorns and brain corals between those columns pass the check. The ground there read 4.1 against a floor of 3.7.
   - Options:
     - Test the whole footprint, e.g. a grid every 6 studs over 66 × 30, or `GetPartBoundsInBox` against `World.Biomes`.
     - Or hide the dressing that overlaps the carcass while it's down.
3. **The Treasure Wheel's chip is a full-width bar** (`G1-chip-full-width-spin6`).
   - The chip measured 1,309 px wide on a 1,592 px screen, with its keys and words at the right end. "free spins full" was clipped at the edge, on both a fresh client and the long solo session.
   - Cause: `Ui.panel` adds a `TopLight` hairline sized `{1, -24}`. In this chip it sits under a horizontal `UIListLayout` with `AutomaticSize.X`, so the hairline takes a slot in the list sized from the chip, and the chip grows to fit it.
   - Fix: destroy the chip's `TopLight` after `Ui.panel` in `TreasureWheelController` `buildHint`, or put the list in an inner auto-sized frame.
   - `HintController`'s Hint chip is built the same way (`Ui.panel` + `UIListLayout` + `AutomaticSize.X`), so it probably does the same. I didn't see it this round. `HudController`'s CursorChip has no list layout.
4. **S1's 'Cuda and Angler skins don't exist.**
   - Only 7 shades carry a sheen: Diamond, Divine and Prismatic (Nibbler), Aurora and Nebula (Reef Shark), Exotic (Pufferfish) and Void (Moray).
   - So the 'Cuda and Angler can never wear a sheen, and their teeth are safe.
   - The toothed fish that can are the Reef Shark (Nebula, Aurora) and the Moray (Void), and all three were checked.
